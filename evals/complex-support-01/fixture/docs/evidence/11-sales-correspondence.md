# E11 — Sales correspondence and commercial representations

Record version: CRM-218.2. Owner: Sales Operations. Messages dated: 2024-01-26 through 2024-03-01. Status: correspondence and unaccepted proposal; authority varies by sender.

January 26 — Account executive Felix Rowan told Mara that Agency Basic was suitable for small client sites and that unusual usage could be reviewed with a person. Felix also wrote that HarborEdge would “keep the sites available,” using broad language without explaining the metered exposure. The signed order later included usage charges and security exceptions. The correspondence matters to reasonable reliance and the relationship, but Felix did not sign an unlimited-spend indemnity or an availability guarantee. Support should acknowledge the gap in explanation instead of insisting that a signed order erases the practical effect of sales language.

January 29 — Sales engineer Ada Kim described alerts and manual review. Ada's technical notes say thresholds notify staff and customers; they do not stop transfer. The email thread does not show that Mara opened the attached technical notes. Ada's role permits explaining existing features and configuring a demonstration, not modifying financial obligations. The two sales messages therefore differ in precision and authority. A faithful response should neither pretend the broad assurance never happened nor convert it into a feature that the product lacks.

February 27 — Felix proposed Team Plus at $4,800 annually, including a larger transfer allowance, priority routing to support, and an architecture review. The proposal's cover note suggested this would put the overage problem behind Aster. The detailed quote says existing invoices remain subject to their own review and continued use above the new allowance is metered. No ceiling on spend is included. The quote expires March 4 but the sales manager can reissue it; expiry is a pricing workflow fact, not a reason to pressure a distressed customer into acceptance today.

March 1 — Sales manager Jules Orr approved a ten-percent annual subscription discount if Mara elects Team Plus after receiving the corrected feature explanation. Jules cannot approve fee forgiveness, cash refunds, or contract releases. The discount applies to future subscription price only. It neither reduces the incident receivable nor returns the $600 debit. Offering it as if it solves the immediate dispute would merge unrelated decisions and conceal the continuing exposure.

The account executive's commission is recognized when the annual term is accepted. That incentive is a reason for clear boundaries and independent billing authority. It does not prove dishonest intent. Felix has asked Support to schedule a short joint call before renewal. Support can offer the call after the billing update, with the customer's consent, without making a purchase or a call a condition of relief. Mara may reasonably prefer to stay monthly, leave, or postpone architecture work.

Sales Operations asks that all written follow-up identify the actual benefits: larger included allowance, named response routing, and scheduled design advice. Avoid promising guaranteed prevention, retroactive coverage, unlimited resources, or an automatic cap. If a commercial option is mentioned, explain the price, future scope, optional nature, and unresolved limits in plain terms. The billing owner retains responsibility for the dispute even when Sales participates.
