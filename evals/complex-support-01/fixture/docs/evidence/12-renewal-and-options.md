# E12 — Renewal choices and account continuity

Record version: REV-482.1. Owner: Revenue Operations. Prepared: 2024-03-01 09:45 UTC. Status: options analysis, not a customer election. Current monthly renewal: 2024-03-04 00:00 UTC.

Aster can remain on Agency Basic at $120 monthly, accept an appropriately authorized Team Plus offer, or request nonrenewal and an export plan. None of these choices automatically resolves the incident invoice. The director's incident adjustment can be processed independently of whether Aster remains a customer. Customer consent must cover the specific subscription change; asking what Team Plus would do is not acceptance. Silence before a sales quote expires is not acceptance of an annual term.

Keeping the existing monthly plan preserves the current hosting arrangement and avoids an immediate annual commitment. It leaves a shared allowance and metered usage beyond it. Targeted asset removal and separate large-media hosting could lower risk without a plan purchase. A proposal to stay monthly should describe those practical limits, especially if the customer assumes the dispute hold also blocks future usage charges. The hold protects collection on the incident; new service use still needs management.

The unaccepted Team Plus quote includes more transfer and support routing. It might fit a future campaign, but no available projection shows that its allowance would have absorbed the incident's scale. An annual purchase does not make the historical invoice disappear or transform monitoring into enforcement. A useful recommendation would compare ordinary and campaign usage after the incident explanation, rather than calculate a seductive savings number from incomplete traffic samples. Sales has not supplied an approved custom spending limit.

Nonrenewal requires Mara's authenticated instruction. Operations can schedule it for the monthly boundary and arrange a short export bridge if the duty lead approves the service impact and finance records the charges or courtesy waiver explicitly. An export bridge is not unlimited production hosting. It should state which sites remain publicly available, which administrative access stays open, how long logs are retained, and who verifies the customer has a usable copy. Export of source repositories hosted elsewhere should be described accurately.

Aster's Friday client briefing creates pressure, but a rushed cancellation would not safely protect Beacon or Cedar. If Mara prefers to leave, Support should offer a clear decision path and ask for scope and timing. It should not infer cancellation from frustration. If Mara stays undecided at the cutoff, the responder can state the current renewal date, offer help with a choice, and arrange a bounded collection extension where necessary. The operational plan must not secretly submit nonrenewal while the external reply says no decision is needed.

Revenue Operations can provision an architecture consultation next Tuesday without requiring an annual sale. The ordinary monthly fee remains payable unless a separate authorized courtesy adjustment is recorded. This option may help rebuild trust while preserving flexibility. The team should be candid if a better long-term fit involves a different architecture or provider. Commercial judgment includes helping the customer avoid another unsuitable purchase, not maximizing immediate contract value at the moment of highest fear.
