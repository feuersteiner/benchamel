# E15 — Cancellation, export, and incident information handling

Policy version: DATA-3.4. Owners: Customer Operations and Privacy. Effective: 2024-02-10. Status: current for self-service agency accounts. This is a fictional operating procedure, not legal advice about any real provider.

The account owner may request nonrenewal, immediate termination, site-specific suspension, or an export-only transition. These are distinct instructions with different effects. Staff must confirm the requested scope and effective time in the authenticated thread. A threat to leave if a dispute continues is not consent to execute termination. An administrator's deployment permission does not authorize destruction of the organization's retained logs or changes to the financial contract.

Before planned termination, provide an inventory of deployment artifacts, redirects, configuration, and operational records available for export. Source repositories hosted elsewhere remain under Aster's own control, and registrar-held domains remain with the registrar. Do not promise a complete source backup if HarborEdge only holds built artifacts. The export package should be checked for usability, not merely marked delivered because a download link exists. Identify any unsupported reconstruction work before scheduling the cutoff.

The ordinary retention period after termination is thirty days for account artifacts, except shorter-lived logs whose original retention remains applicable. The preservation receipt P-31 places the identified incident sample under a case hold through March 15 unless Privacy extends it. Closing a subscription must not silently release that preservation hold. Conversely, retaining one incident sample does not justify retaining all downstream client visitor data indefinitely. Record the lawful task basis and limit access to staff working the incident.

The agency may receive its account-level usage and appropriately minimized request information. Individual visitor identifiers, unrelated downstream client traffic, and third-party account information need Privacy review. An account owner cannot authorize disclosure of another HarborEdge customer's raw data merely by requesting it. Redaction should preserve useful technical explanation where possible, including time windows, aggregate counts, requested asset classes, and known limitations. A refusal to send raw visitor addresses should come with a safe alternative and a review owner.

Export access may be kept available during a negotiated transition while public serving is paused or restricted by consent. That arrangement requires a written scope, duration, and any charges or courtesy waiver. It must not be disguised as a normal active subscription if essential client pages will disappear. The support owner should check the client briefing schedule and ask how the customer wants to handle continuity, rather than presume a one-size-fits-all exit.

Billing relief does not require deletion, silence, a release of claims, or abandoning the information request under the current approval. Aster can accept an invoice correction and still ask for the incident explanation. If Legal later proposes a separate settlement agreement, staff must identify it as a new proposal and allow an authorized signatory to review it. The present plan should not invent that agreement or withhold the approved cash return until the customer signs an unrelated document.
