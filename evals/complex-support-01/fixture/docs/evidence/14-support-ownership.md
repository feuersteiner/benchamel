# E14 — Support ownership and escalation procedure

Policy version: SUP-6.0. Owner: Head of Support. Effective: 2024-02-22. Status: current. Case H-482 owner at cutoff: Niko Reed, duty support lead, supported by associate Daria Moss.

The owner coordinates billing, engineering, security, privacy, and commercial follow-up. Assigning specialist work does not transfer responsibility for updating the customer. The owner must state the next update time and provide a fallback if a specialist cannot finish. A technically unresolved account can still receive a complete and responsible customer response. The question is whether the reply makes accurate commitments and advances the case, not whether every underlying question has been closed.

Mara requested an update before 17:00 UTC on March 1. Niko can commit to a written update by 16:30 and an interim message earlier if a posting window is missed. The next update should say what has actually changed, what is still pending, and when the next controlled action will happen. A promise of a final security report by that time would exceed the team's current evidence. A security progress update is acceptable if it preserves the difference between progress and attribution.

Before sending, the owner checks the ledger snapshot, approval scope, collection expiry, customer consent, and known contradictory records. The purpose is to find material gaps rather than demand ceremonial sign-off from every department. Internal records already contain the account number, settled debit, relevant deployment, and preservation receipt. Staff should not send Mara on a search for those facts. A narrow question about the intent or scope of an agency script may be necessary because only Aster can answer it; the owner should explain why it matters.

A duty lead can extend the incident hold through March 8 at 18:00 UTC by recording a new decision and confirming the collection state. If the financial remedy is posted sooner, close the redundant incident collection task after checking that no stale reminder or debit remains queued. A scheduled extension is an action plan until the collection system confirms it. Customer wording must reflect that state. Future ordinary subscription renewal requires its own explanation and consent handling.

For technical changes, the owner must attach the customer's specific authorization and engineering's rollback plan. The available authorization covers Linden's asset replacement, not a full-account pause. If Engineering believes a broader change is necessary, obtain informed owner consent or escalate a documented emergency. Do not bury the broader change in internal shorthand such as “protect account” while reassuring the customer that essential information sites remain available.

Case closure requires an accurate outcome record, a customer update, and assigned owners for any surviving technical or privacy work. An approved invoice remedy does not close a request for incident information. A pending investigation does not justify leaving collection unprotected. If Mara chooses to leave, continue export support under the cancellation procedure. Disagreement over a contract interpretation should go to the named decision maker with the conflicting evidence attached, a bounded review time, and a safe interim account state.
