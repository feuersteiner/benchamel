That is material to remediation but does not justify blaming him or concealing ValeGrid's broad sales answer.

The customer was subscribed to subprocessor notices when SignalArc was added August 20. A notice was sent July 17 to the recorded governance role mailbox; the mail system recorded delivery acceptance. There is no read receipt or evidence of a specific human reviewing it. The notice described security event metadata, not customer document content, and linked the DPA objection procedure. Bracken did not submit an objection in the portal. This supports dispatch and server acceptance; it does not prove informed acceptance of every telemetry field or settle whether notice satisfied a stricter annex obligation.

PageSplice told Privacy at 09:40 today that its account-level report is normally available within two business days. Current configuration shows a seven-day derivative cache, but the vendor has not yet confirmed whether failed jobs produce longer diagnostic copies. It can receive a suspension and deletion inquiry through the authenticated integration owner channel. ValeGrid may forward minimized job identifiers under existing processing arrangements. Sending the full disputed spreadsheet to prove a residency problem would create unnecessary new processing. Provider confirmations must identify scope, time and remaining retention conditions.

A new EU support routing option exists for designated cases during EU working hours. It does not replace the global ticket store or certify every escalation participant's location. BeaconSpan can be excluded from this active case while access logs are checked, using an approved routing tag. The control limits future assignment; it is not evidence about past access. Privacy owns a matrix of processor, category, location, retention and account-specific evidence, rather than one undifferentiated statement that subprocessors are approved and therefore irrelevant.
