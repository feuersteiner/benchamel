# E10 — Superseded regional sales and migration guide

Record version: REG-3.0. All ValeGrid and Bracken Municipal Services records are synthetic.

Owner: Enablement archive. Issued May 2025; superseded July 1, 2026 by REG-5.2 in E09. The guide remains searchable in the sales portal with an archived badge that was hidden in one mobile view. It says regional workspaces keep your data close to home and includes a template answer marking EU-only hosting as yes. Its introductory definition of data refers to content stores. Later pages mention that account identity, support records and optional integrations follow their own arrangements. The short template does not carry that distinction into the questionnaire answer.

The guide's standard move example uses a newly created sandbox containing 25 GB of synthetic data and no integrations. It gives a four-hour estimate for that example and a sample Friday cutover. A footnote requires Engineering to estimate each production tenant. The example does not cover a 2.4 TB content estate, immutable backups, customer-managed endpoint dependencies or a third-party derivative cache. The copy distributed to Lina in September 2025 included the footnote, but her September 2026 message used the example's timing as a promise. The archive establishes how the error could arise, not permission to repeat it.

The former process allowed an account executive to request a region evidence letter from Service Governance after a specialist review. It never delegated signature of an all-data compliance certificate to Sales. One sample letter said content remains in the chosen location subject to described operational exceptions. The sample's exception page was a required attachment. It cannot support sending only the reassuring first paragraph. The version now available in the portal has a reminder that earlier examples may not reflect current product behavior; that later annotation does not prove a customer saw it when buying.

A capability table listed an EU telemetry pilot with a target of Q3 2026. The word target was explicit, with availability subject to validation. The current engineering record has not declared it generally available for global identity or historic support attachments. An old roadmap target therefore cannot be converted into a promise that a single switch makes every processing category regional this week. No archived guide author had case authority to delete backups, restrict mandatory security monitoring, or amend a signed DPA.

Training staff are collecting the specific versions that generated the account's answers, while Product Marketing corrects live collateral. Keeping this archived record is necessary to understand the prior representation and internal incentive; it is not current operational authority. The customer must not be asked to choose between accepting a misleading old template and abandoning service immediately. Current support can correct facts and set bounded work while Commercial Legal examines the signed documents. Archive provenance is a separate issue from the customer remedy, and staff should not claim the guide's retirement alone repaired Bracken's reliance or removed existing copies.
