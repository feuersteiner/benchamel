# E09 — Current region claims and support delegation

Record version: REG-5.2. All ValeGrid and Bracken Municipal Services records are synthetic.

Owner: Service Governance. Effective July 1, 2026. Policy applies to new public statements and ongoing account support; it does not unilaterally narrow a previously executed customer annex. EU Content Region means designated application content stores and routine production recovery replicas reside in the configured EU locations. Global account administration, security processing, optional integrations and support artifacts must be described separately. Staff must use the scoped name in location explanations and correct an overbroad earlier representation when account evidence establishes one. Security safeguards and contractual transfer measures must not be described as physical residence.

A support lead may state verified present-state facts, open a processor inquiry, apply an approved future-case routing tag, arrange existing exports and coordinate reversible configuration tests. The lead may explain that contractual interpretation remains under counsel review. The lead cannot certify general legal compliance, amend the region annex, promise a new architecture, promise erasure across third-party backups, or guarantee the absence of past foreign access. A statement that source documents are in Frankfurt and Dublin is materially different from a certificate that all service data has always stayed in the EU.

Operations approval is required for production migration and any change that reduces diagnostic or recovery capability. Customer operational consent must identify the affected service, window, expected downtime, rollback boundary and unresolved copy scope. A preliminary quotation or attendance at a planning call is not that consent. The technical approver verifies readiness; the customer controls its business tolerance; Legal controls amendments. One approval does not substitute for the others. A successful dry run using synthetic fixtures cannot establish production completion or erase historical processing.

Finance may offer a remedy only within a recorded delegation or case approval. A product move made necessary by an inaccurate sales assurance must not be sold as a compliance guarantee to recover revenue. Commercial options may be discussed after their limits are explained, including continued service under documented interim restrictions, transition assistance or a separately scoped design engagement. The policy does not dictate which option Bracken must choose. Support must not condition factual disclosures, preservation or a standard export on renewal, public silence, a release, or purchase of a higher tier.

Routing exceptions must be logged with an expiry and an owner so global automation does not silently reassign the case. Case summaries should track observed states rather than rely on the console badge. A customer update must distinguish booked work from requested work, attempted configuration from verified state, and residual data from live primary content. If a promised milestone is missed, the named case owner gives a useful update at that milestone with the obstacle, interim state and next decision time. Waiting for every specialist to finish is not a substitute for owning an imminent customer meeting.
