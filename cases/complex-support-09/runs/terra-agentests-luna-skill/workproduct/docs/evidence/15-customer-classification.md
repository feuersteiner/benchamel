The September 17 supplier review requires a written category matrix and a practical decision record. Hana says the panel can consider temporary exceptions if Selma sponsors a documented risk decision, but she cannot predict or guarantee its approval. The panel has asked whether global identity information can remain outside the EU when operational content stays regional. This is a real question, not a granted exemption. An unconditional EU-only certificate would prevent the panel from seeing the issue it must decide. Conversely, a generic statement that cloud services are global would give the panel no usable evidence.

Pavel's local worksheet can receive new work descriptions and reference IDs, but not attachments or live supplier confirmations. A read-only ValeGrid period would allow staff to retrieve historic evidence while entering new changes locally for later reconciliation. The workaround depends on permission checks, a clear marker for new work, and a named person reconciling updates. It is feasible for a short bridge but costs around three staff-hours daily. A complete shutdown today would prevent the final inspection package from being assembled before September 18 and is not requested.

Hana asks the team to identify whether proposed changes reduce exposure, improve evidence, or eliminate a category. Those outcomes should not be conflated. Stripping a diagnostic field reduces content leakage into telemetry; it does not move remaining telemetry. Disabling PDF Preview stops new connector jobs; it does not delete old derivatives. Export gives Bracken an independent working copy; it does not itself terminate the subscription. Bracken's governance obligations remain its responsibility, but ValeGrid's answer must be accurate enough for that responsibility to be exercised. This note establishes classification and dependence, without supplying consent for a production move or a final acceptance of current processing.
