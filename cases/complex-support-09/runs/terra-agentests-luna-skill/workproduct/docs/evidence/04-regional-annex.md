# E04 — Regional annex and contract ambiguity

Record version: ANNEX-EU.1. All ValeGrid and Bracken Municipal Services records are synthetic.

Owner: Commercial Legal archive. Executed September 30, 2025 with the account order. The annex says ValeGrid will maintain service data in the European Union for the regional workspace and use reasonable technical measures to prevent relocation outside that area except on documented instruction. It then identifies application content using documents, attachments and application records as examples. A final sentence says processing details and authorized subprocessors remain governed by the DPA unless expressly varied by this annex. Neither sentence defines whether service data includes every log, support attachment, account profile or transient diagnostic record.

The attached processing matrix places application content in EU hosting, customer account administration under the global service function, and support interactions under case handling. It contains a notes column reading regional configuration agreed. That note spans merged cells for content and diagnostics in the signed PDF, whereas the editable draft had the note beside content only. Archive staff have verified the signed PDF bytes and both signatures. They cannot determine from formatting whether the merged cell was a deliberate negotiation or a layout error. There is no oral-negotiation recording. Counsel requests the final redline correspondence rather than treating either interpretation as self-proving.

DPA Schedule B allows authorized subprocessors to perform security, support and infrastructure services in listed countries under the specified safeguards. It describes customer instructions and objection procedures. General authorization does not establish that a particular attachment was processed abroad, and it does not itself decide how a bespoke later restriction narrows that authorization. The DPA requires notice of a new subprocessor thirty days before planned processing, with a reasonable objection process. It does not say that silence universally waives a separately agreed region term. The current dispute involves both established providers and a connector that Bracken enabled itself.

The master terms' entire-agreement provision does not give frontline staff authority to declare that every sales statement is irrelevant. It also does not make an account executive a general amendment signatory. Reliance on a sales statement, the scope of the signed annex, and a remedy for an inaccurate operational assertion are related questions requiring different evidence. The contract's fee limitation and liability provisions are not reproduced as a ready-made answer to a factual location request. No immediate compensation award has been approved in this record.

Bracken can request a processing explanation and discuss remediation without executing a release. A letter purporting to certify the whole service against all territorial requirements would create a new representation. The annex supplies no standard form for that certification. A precise present-state statement can describe verified categories and dates without deciding disputed legal meaning. That distinction does not authorize hiding a known exception behind contractual ambiguity. Whether the regional annex was already breached remains open at the cutoff, while the observed existence of a non-EU ticket attachment is documented separately.
