# E02 — Account, signed order and stakeholder scope

Record version: ORD-416.2. All ValeGrid and Bracken Municipal Services records are synthetic.

Owner: Account Operations. Executed: September 30, 2025. Current term ends September 30, 2026 at 23:59 UTC. Retrieved internally September 15, 2026 at 09:00 UTC. Bracken Municipal Services coordinates planned building maintenance for twelve local service partnerships. Its ValeGrid workspace contains work descriptions, inspection photographs, supplier quotes, building contact details and completion evidence. The workspace is used by 184 staff and 37 invited contractors. It does not dispatch emergency services. A service interruption could still delay inspections and statutory record production; the customer decides the operational tolerance.

The annual Enterprise Regional subscription costs EUR 96,000 excluding taxes. The September 30 renewal invoice has been drafted but not issued, and no payment has been taken for the next term. The signed order lists EU Content Region as an included capability. An attached matrix marks documents, attachments and application databases as regional. The box beside operational metadata refers to DPA Schedule B. No marked box expressly addresses foreign employee access. The customer and account executive initialed the matrix; the sales engineer did not sign the order.

Bracken procurement director Selma Ortiz signed the order and can amend subscriptions, approve commercial terms, authorize export recipients and instruct cancellation. Technology manager Pavel Ross can manage members, disable optional connectors and approve a tested maintenance window up to four hours. Pavel cannot waive territorial requirements, approve foreign processing exceptions, settle contract claims or cancel the subscription. Bracken data governance officer Hana Bell can classify data and advise procurement; she has not been delegated purchasing or settlement authority. An authenticated message from Selma may authorize a narrowly stated operation without authorizing every action discussed in the same thread.

ValeGrid's account entity is an EU company. That fact does not determine all processing locations. The order references the master services terms dated August 2025 and DPA version 4.1, together with a bespoke two-page regional annex. Order-specific terms take priority for the subject they expressly cover. The annex itself gives no clause-by-clause precedence rule against the DPA. Electronic amendments require both parties' designated signatories; ordinary support correspondence can record operational consent but cannot silently rewrite the annex.

Bracken retains ownership of source documents. ValeGrid must supply standard exports during an active subscription and for thirty days after expiry, subject to authentication and the documented export process. Termination, deletion and export are separate instructions. There is no data-residency certification deliverable named in the order, although the regional annex calls for a reasonable evidence statement on request. Bracken says that statement was a central purchase condition. Account Operations has found no later executed amendment that defines the disputed phrase service data more precisely.
