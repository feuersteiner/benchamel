# E11 — Engineering migration assessment and blockers

Record version: MOVE-914.3. All ValeGrid and Bracken Municipal Services records are synthetic.

Owner: Dev Malik. Assessment September 16, 2026 at 10:30 UTC. Bracken has 2.4 TB of active attachments, 1.8 million application records and twelve downstream connector mappings. Primary content is already in EU stores. The proposed work is isolation into a dedicated EU content tenancy, replacement of global telemetry with a scoped EU collector, separate regional support handling, and disposition of legacy artifacts. These are distinct work packages. The content isolation would improve evidencing and access separation but cannot on its own satisfy an all-service-data and all-personnel territorial requirement.

A production move needs a verified mapping of object keys to job records, destination capacity reservation, link translation and a customer-side endpoint test. The standard export preserves original job identifiers, attachment checksums and user-role references, but private preview URLs must be regenerated. Existing webhook secrets cannot simply be copied into the destination; Bracken must rotate them using its own key owner. The only completed rehearsal used 80 GB of synthetic fixtures. It failed two supplier acknowledgement links because an old connector embedded absolute workspace URLs. That defect is understood but the fix has not passed a second rehearsal.

The earliest engineering planning estimate is September 23 for a supported content-isolation window, subject to a passing rehearsal on September 18 and customer approval. Expected read-only time is six to eight hours, with an upper planning limit of twelve hours. That exceeds Pavel's delegated four-hour window. No zero-downtime commitment is available. The operation cannot finish before the September 17 supplier review. A rushed direct database copy might move rows faster but would bypass object association checks, retention rules and the supported rollback boundary. Dev has rejected it as an unapproved procedure, not a customer-selectable shortcut.

EU telemetry collector testing can proceed without production document content and is expected to yield an assessment by September 18 at 14:00. Disabling the global stream before validation would remove alerts needed to detect failed writes and suspicious access. A reduced-field mode approved for this tenant strips the free-text validation field while preserving event IDs and timing; it can be staged today. It does not make the remaining telemetry EU-resident. Global identity still has no regional replacement. Historic ticket storage and PageSplice copies remain separate inquiries owned by Privacy.

Operations needs a go/no-go owner and customer consent before any production cutover is booked. The destination may be reserved provisionally without moving data or extending the subscription. Failed rehearsal means no cutover, a revised estimate and continued work on current protections or export. Success means technical eligibility, not customer acceptance or legal certification. The migration estimate must remain visible as a conditional planning forecast. There is no authorized commitment to a September 18 pure EU stack, and no evidence that paying for the renewal accelerates a blocker involving unsupported identity architecture.
