This review began as a residency and representation dispute, not a confirmed malicious intrusion. Data-protection notification duties are under counsel and Privacy review; support cannot infer that no legal assessment is required simply because access may have been by an authorized supplier. The attachment and access summaries are under preservation ticket PRES-914. It records hashes of the captured internal artifacts, custodian names and a restricted access list. Those private technical artifacts are not in this public fictional fixture. Operational preservation in the scenario can coexist with a customer's request to remove unnecessary copies, but changing retention requires Privacy to identify what can be removed without destroying relevant evidence. No staff member has authority to delete audit events to make the account appear clean. A migration change record must cite the preservation ticket and separate live copies, archived evidence and backups.

Mei can provide a customer-safe account chronology today by 17:00 and a clearer coverage statement by September 18 at 12:00 UTC. The chronology should name processing countries and purposes where verified, without employee identifiers, shared-tenant records or detection rules. An incomplete audit record limits certainty; it does not require withholding all current locations until a final forensic conclusion that may never be attainable.
