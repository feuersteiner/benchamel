# E12 — Continuity, nonrenewal and export procedure

Record version: CONT-2025.2. Owner: Customer Operations. Effective: 2025-05-01. Case assessment: 2025-06-27 11:00 UTC. Status: current service operations procedure. All business records, people, dialogue and account identifiers in this dossier are synthetic.

A manual renewal gate preserves the existing Core40 entitlement for its recorded period. It prevents the automatic renewal workflow from deciding the disputed price while a responsible owner resolves the next instruction. Under the duty-lead delegation, up to seven calendar days of this continuity beyond the paid boundary can be provided without a new charge; the processing gate can be entered before that boundary. This is a processing accommodation, not a fresh annual sale. It does not close projects, reduce staff count or cancel Rookfield's existing order. The gate's expiry must be set deliberately because the batch engine otherwise retries queued work.

When a customer elects a paid bridge, Finance must replace the annual draft with the approved short-period schedule and record that the annual renewal path is not to resume at gate expiry. Customer Operations verifies that service entitlement covers the accepted bridge dates. The bridge's end does not authorize automatic conversion to the $14,400 annual plan. A separate election must define what follows. At least ten calendar days before bridge expiry, the owner must contact the commercial signatory with accurate options and transition implications.

An authorized nonrenewal instruction can stop the upcoming annual term. It must specify whether full production access ends at the paid boundary, a separately approved bridge ends later, or an export interval is requested. Nonrenewal is different from immediate deletion. Immediate account cancellation removes ordinary user access and can interrupt active workflows, so it must not be inferred from a disputed price or a request to stop an automatic charge. The archive-retention schedule is thirty days from actual account closure; a dispute gate does not start that clock.

Standard exports include projects and uploaded attachments. External-drive files remain links, and users need their own access to retrieve them. Export ownership rests with Noor, with Support coordinating verification of a sample. Staff should not promise complete migration from file generation alone. Rookfield's integration owners need to validate mapping of project identifiers and scheduled jobs in any replacement system. Customer Operations estimates two working days for a full export job after authorized scoping, subject to unusually large attachments. No guarantee of a complete competitor migration by July 1 is supported.

A reduced tier can only follow a clear scope and impact review. Team30 would remove capabilities Rookfield currently uses and has fewer seats than active staff. The product's public list price is not evidence of customer consent to that downgrade. Any proposal must state what will stop, when, and what preparation is needed. If the customer remains undecided, the owner must give the actual gate expiry and next decision checkpoint rather than imply indefinite free service. Project continuity has commercial value and operating cost, and it should be described candidly without turning the current dispute into fear-based pressure.
