# E04 — Historical and current signatory register

Record version: AUTH-2022.4. Owner: Corporate Secretary. Register snapshot: 2025-06-27 13:15 UTC. Status: historical delegation and signature scope; not a new decision. All business records, people, dialogue and account identifiers in this dossier are synthetic.

In June 2022, an account executive could issue the approved annual price schedule and offer a first-term discount up to twenty percent. A regional sales manager could approve a discount for the current annual term. An indefinite price lock, a variation of renewal-price provisions, or a commitment lasting more than two years required the chief commercial officer and finance director jointly. The register contains no delegation that converted account-executive status into general authority to bind all future renewals.

Regional manager Petra Venn signed the implementation acceptance certificate on June 16, 2022. The certificate confirms readiness of onboarding resources and approval of the commercial configuration in order NF-2240. Its signature field is headed delivery and first-term commercial approval. A checkbox states price schedule reviewed. The certificate does not reproduce the rate-assurance wording. Rookfield retained it alongside Ava's assurance; Miri reads Petra's signature as company confirmation of the whole deal. Contracts staff read the certificate as confirming the implementation and first annual term. Neither view changes the words that the certificate actually contains.

The historical register was internal. The customer did not receive a copy in 2022. Counsel has cautioned that internal delegation limits and the effect of customer-facing representations are related but different questions. The register identifies who may authorize today's concessions; it is not itself a final adjudication of apparent authority, incorporation or reasonable reliance. Staff should not tell the customer that a representation is automatically void simply because a salesperson lacked an internal code. They also should not represent that Petra expressly approved a permanent price when the signature scope does not say that.

The current authority schedule, AUTH-2025.1, took effect April 1, 2025. Support associates may explain records and coordinate a case. Duty support leads may establish a manual renewal gate before the batch runs, deferring the scheduled renewal for up to seven calendar days beyond the paid boundary and preserving existing service during that extension without an additional charge. This delegation changes processing and short continuity, not the permanent negotiated price or the customer's election. Finance Operations can alter a draft invoice once a financial approval and any required acceptance are attached. It cannot create a perpetual price exception or use a renewal gate as a cancellation instruction.

Finance director Sela Moss may approve up to one additional annual Core40 term at the legacy price, or a paid bridge up to three calendar months at the corresponding legacy monthly rate. She cannot approve a permanent price guarantee, a waiver of all claims, or an unpriced forty-eight-seat account. Joint executive and legal review would be needed for a new indefinite commitment or a formal dispute settlement. The June 27 case-specific decision in E15 is distinct from this general delegation. All staff must use its actual conditions rather than infer approval from a title alone.
