# E06 — Current pricing change and renewal procedure

Record version: PRICE-2025.1. Owner: Revenue Operations. Issued: 2025-03-15. Effective: 2025-04-01 for new orders; existing renewals subject to contract and notice. Status: current operating policy. All business records, people, dialogue and account identifiers in this dossier are synthetic.

The standardized annual Core40 list price is $14,400 excluding tax. This policy applies immediately to new orders signed on or after April 1. Existing negotiated accounts must be checked against their order, any relevant representation, and the applicable notice requirement. A general policy date is not a universal effective date for every customer. For an existing account with a sixty-day price-notice requirement, staff must record the intended renewal boundary and evidence of the prescribed notice before treating the increase as undisputed.

NacreFlow's notice standard distinguishes dispatch, server acceptance, content coverage and human acknowledgment. A mail-service success code can establish that a receiving system accepted a message. It does not prove that a customer read it, that the correct contractual address was used, or that the message described the relevant amount and effective date. A dashboard banner is supplemental unless the applicable agreement makes it the notice channel. Support can report observed evidence without adopting a categorical legal conclusion about effectiveness.

Auto-renewal settings reflect the existing order's renewal mechanism. They do not authorize staff to accept an unexecuted change on the customer's behalf. When a material price dispute remains near renewal, the owner should obtain a duty-lead processing gate or an approved commercial instruction before the automatic batch runs. The gate must state an expiry, service entitlement and follow-up owner. It is not a cancellation, a refund or a silent replacement contract. Leaving a gate to expire without a plan can cause the old automation to resume.

A paid bridge is a separately described short subscription interval. It requires authorized customer acceptance of price, dates, service scope and what happens at its end. Staff must not call a bridge free if it has a charge or imply that accepting it resolves permanent-rate claims. A one-year commercial accommodation also requires the necessary provider approval and customer authority. It does not prove the historical letter was invalid or valid. Records should distinguish a without-prejudice operational accommodation from a legally final settlement.

Support must not condition preservation of a disputed claim on purchasing an expansion or deleting public criticism. No standard cancellation fee applies before the pending renewal in this account. Nonrenewal and export can be offered accurately, but a customer expression of dissatisfaction is not enough to close a working account. The owner must record the specific election. If a negotiated resolution is still pending, keep the status open, make the next controlled update, and refer the actual authority issue to the named decision maker. Current policy does not require staff to make an unsupported final legal determination in order to prevent a disputed automatic charge.
