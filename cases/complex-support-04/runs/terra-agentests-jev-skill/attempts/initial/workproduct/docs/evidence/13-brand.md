# E13 — Customer communication and public claims guide

Record version: COM-2025.4. Owner: Communications. Effective: 2025-05-15. Status: current general guidance. All business records, people, dialogue and account identifiers in this dossier are synthetic.

NacreFlow's public positioning emphasizes predictable costs and dependable planning. That positioning creates a reason to explain a substantial change carefully; it is not a source of contract authority. The support team should acknowledge when earlier communication was incomplete, confusing or contradicted by an authentic archived message. A clear correction should identify the incorrect statement and give the verified replacement facts. It should not require the customer to accept a new plan as a condition of receiving an explanation.

Avoid shorthand such as everyone was notified, the system proves you agreed, all old promises are void, or nothing will ever change again. These statements combine multiple factual and legal questions in ways the record may not support. Describe the evidence at its actual scope. A message accepted by a server, a profile-address change, a dashboard view and a signature have different meanings. Technical terms should be translated when they affect a financial choice. The customer needs to understand what has been sent, what is planned and what remains undecided.

A pricing explanation may discuss investment and operating costs if it is consistent with approved company material. It must not present a generic cost argument as proof that a particular contractual increase is effective. The cooperative does not need to agree that the new price is good value before its notice concern can be investigated. Nor should the company concede every legal theory in order to show respect. A response can acknowledge reliance, describe a pending interpretation and keep service safe within recorded authority.

Avoid performative familiarity, defensive language about loyalty, and threats to delete data when no closure has been requested. Do not blame a departed salesperson, a customer contractor or a support associate for all uncertainty. The company owns the explanation and correction. It may describe a stale contact snapshot without naming an individual mailbox operator. All public-facing statements about an account require the account owner's consent and the ordinary privacy check. A private support resolution does not authorize publication of a customer story.

A useful written message leads with the present account state, the controlled next action and the choice that remains with the customer. Exact amounts and dates should be understandable without reading an internal ticket. If a proposed accommodation is temporary, name its end and what it does not decide. If a review is pending, name the owner and next update. A warm tone cannot compensate for a hidden invoice action, and operational precision need not be cold. Support should correct the sales impression about expansion and legacy status directly enough that the customer can decide without assuming a purchase is necessary to keep the dispute open.
