# E13 — Service terms and counsel intake

Record version: LEG-619.1. Owner: counsel Rina Cho. Dated: 2025-05-14 10:55 UTC. Status: fictional contract summary and internal guidance; not a liability adjudication or new financial approval.

The signed order incorporates service terms dated May 1, 2024. They require Flint Ledger to maintain reasonable backup and recovery measures and give service credits for specified availability failures. They distinguish availability credits from other claims and contain a limitation clause whose application depends on claim type and circumstances. This internal summary does not establish that all data-loss claims are excluded. The customer has cited reliance on Sales and deficient recovery controls. Support should preserve those issues for the authorized reviewers rather than tell Meridian that the terms conclusively eliminate all responsibility.

The order includes a one-hour recovery-point objective and an eight-hour recovery-time objective for infrastructure disaster recovery. These describe the tested design for a defined infrastructure failure, not an unconditional per-workspace undo guarantee. Whether this maintenance event falls within the defined service failure is under review. A technical objective cannot be used as proof that a one-hour-old usable backup exists, and a limitation in that objective does not excuse the unsupported two-hour promise. Facts about the present artifacts and work remain necessary.

Rina has reviewed CA-619 and confirms it creates a no-release May fee remedy and a bounded reconstruction expense allowance. It does not authorize lost-tender compensation or settle liability. Counsel cannot increase the director's spending limit. The customer should not have to give up a claim or suppress a public complaint to receive the authorized fee refund. Staff may ask for a concise basis and supporting expense records; they should not make every recovery update conditional on a complete damages dossier.

There is a genuine classification question if a supplier charges for reserving capacity rather than delivering reconstruction. A signed work order and nonrefundability can establish that a cost was incurred, but they do not necessarily establish that its substance is reconstruction expense. Conversely, a fixed-fee diagnostic can be reconstruction work even if it yields no recovered records. Nessa must interpret the allowance against the actual supplier scope. Counsel is available to explain the legal effect of any proposed settlement, not to decide technical data recoverability or certify an invoice it has not seen.

An external account-level update can acknowledge that Flint Ledger's maintenance action removed project relationships, that a usable recent state is not yet established and that earlier assurances were premature. It need not name the operator, publish internal host details or assert permanent destruction before the tests finish. The review should not turn factual uncertainty into vague reassurance. Rina has asked Ellis for a copy of the eventual incident explanation for claim preservation, but does not require preapproval of ordinary truthful operational updates within the established disclosure scope. Final root-cause language and any settlement agreement will receive a separate review.
