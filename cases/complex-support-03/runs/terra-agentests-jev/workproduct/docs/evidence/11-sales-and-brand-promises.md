# E11 — Sales assurances and marketing correction

Record version: CRM-884.3. Owner: account executive Milo Hart. Original exchange: 2025-04-18. Incident annotation: 2025-05-14 10:45 UTC. Status: authenticated sales correspondence and correction request; not a signed service guarantee.

In April, Meridian asked whether the service was suitable for high-stakes bid records. Milo wrote that automated backups meant Meridian would never lose an approval and that the service could be restored within hours. He linked a brand page using the phrase confidence in every decision. The order did not include a zero-loss warranty or a fixed recovery time for accidental metadata deletion. Milo did not obtain a technical review before making those assurances. Their absence from the order does not make the customer's reliance unreasonable or erase the need to correct them.

At 08:45 today Milo told Sera that engineering expected everything back by 15:00. The message expanded Jules's database estimate to include all approval data. At 09:50 Milo asked Support to avoid calling the situation data loss until engineering had exhausted backups, because an annual plan discussion was scheduled for Friday. The recovery team has not accepted that communication restriction. Uncertainty about permanent loss permits a precise description of missing and unverified data; it does not support saying the project is safe or that nothing was lost.

The annual proposal is $28,800 for twelve months beginning May 16 and includes an optional Recovery Assist export feature at no additional charge for the first year. Milo says this is intended to repair confidence. The feature schedules future customer exports; it cannot reconstruct absent historical approval signatures or accelerate the current missing-segment retrieval. No annual order is signed. A free future feature is not the same as cash reimbursement for documented recovery expense. No purchase may be processed on the theory that a distressed customer's silence is acceptance.

Brand manager Keir Wynn reviewed the existing page at 10:15. The general promise can remain as an aspiration, but Keir withdrew a draft banner saying that no customer work is ever lost. A revised page is pending review. Keir asks the incident reply to acknowledge the actual impact and correct previous unsupported assurances in plain language. He has not authorized a statement that all backups are broken, all tenants are affected, or the engineering team guaranteed a morning recovery.

Milo remains responsible for clarifying the April and morning statements with the account owner. Ellis should coordinate that clarification with the recovery update so the customer does not receive competing accounts. Sales may explain future options after giving accurate incident information, but recovery scheduling and approved financial relief are not to depend on retention. Milo can attend the 14:00 board briefing if Sera wants him there. No evidence shows that Sera asked to hear an upgrade presentation in place of an operational plan.
