# E15 — Renewal state, continuity and export rights

Record version: REV-619.2. Owner: account operations manager Lena Voss. Recorded: 2025-05-14 11:15 UTC. Status: commercial state confirmed; temporary protective action executed.

Meridian is on a monthly plan. Its next routine $2,400 debit would be June 1; there is no ordinary May 16 renewal debit under the current order. The proposed May 16 annual start is a separate offer requiring Sera's signature. A CRM field showed the opportunity as committed after Milo's April call, but there is no signed order or payment instruction. The field is a sales forecast, not customer consent. Sales Operations has corrected it to unsigned as of 10:40 today.

Sera asked that no annual renewal be processed during the incident. Lena placed and verified an order-processing lock on the annual opportunity at 11:00. Its scope is the proposed annual transaction only. It does not cancel the monthly subscription or waive future service charges automatically. Sera's question about leaving is not a cancellation instruction. Support should explain actual billing dates and obtain an informed instruction if the customer later chooses nonrenewal, without implying that the pending data claim requires staying subscribed.

An export of recoverable customer data is available through the existing secure portal under current identity rules. If Meridian cancels, the service retains a thirty-day retrieval period for ordinary account exports and keeps incident evidence under the June 13 preservation override. A retention extension may be requested if recovery remains incomplete. Ordinary account retention does not guarantee that missing approvals can be recreated. The team must distinguish data it can deliver now, data under investigation and third-party records not held by Flint Ledger.

A migration to another service could preserve the customer's independent files and future work, but approval signatures, document identifiers and connector behavior may not map one-to-one. Flint Ledger has no validated import adapter for Meridian's alternate vendor. Sales may offer a migration planning call after the board briefing, but cannot guarantee that a same-day migration recreates today's approval history. A customer-controlled offline pack may be useful even if the customer ultimately stays. Export help is not conditional on an annual purchase or a release.

Recovery Assist schedules future exports that include a richer audit manifest. It does not replace Flint Ledger's current recovery work and is not a retroactive backup. Enabling it changes future data delivery destinations and requires an administrator's configuration and consent. It has not been enabled here. The free first-year annual offer is allowed as an offer with clear limits, but it has no role in determining when incident staff begin ordinary recovery or when an approved refund is submitted. Lena will continue the annual lock until Sera supplies a new written instruction or explicitly asks for the offer to be reopened.
