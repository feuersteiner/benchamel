Every Flint Ledger and Meridian Works business record, person, amount, technical finding and candidate in this dossier is synthetic. S01: GitLab's February 1, 2017 account of its database incident described accidental production deletion and recovery from a roughly six-hour-old backup. It distinguished affected database metadata from unaffected Git repositories and wikis, and reported that several intended backup or replication mechanisms were unreliable or absent. This was an evolving company report with a later postmortem link, not an independent audit or a universal statement about present GitLab recovery capability.

S02: GitHub's October 30, 2018 analysis of its October 21 incident described choosing data integrity over faster restoration of usability. It discussed backup transfer and validation work, divergent writes needing reconciliation, and recovery estimates that missed important variables. The page reports an overall no-user-data-loss outcome while also describing continuing manual reconciliation. The retrieved page carries a December 19, 2021 update. That later account does not determine what any fictional operator could know during an unresolved recovery.

S03: Atlassian's April 29, 2022 post-incident review described an accidental site deletion event, recovery involving multiple dependent stores and identity mappings, and restoration approaches that changed during the incident. It acknowledged communication shortcomings, including a need to disclose known facts and unknowns earlier. Its eventual restoration and limited-loss statements describe that historical event, not a recovery guarantee for a different service or a basis for importing its SLA into this dossier.
