# E02 — Account order and project workflow

Record version: ORD-7.2. Owner: account operations analyst Lena Voss. Effective order: 2024-05-16. Account snapshot: 2025-05-14 09:15 UTC. Status: signed fictional order plus verified account inventory; no new recovery consent.

Meridian Works pays Flint Ledger $2,400 monthly for 180 named users and 24 project workspaces. The current month was charged and settled on May 1. Service includes document revision references, structured approval records and export tools. Raw engineering files live in the attachment object store, but an object without its project relationship and revision record may be unusable for evidentiary purposes. The ordinary subscription does not promise that a folder listing is a complete approval register. A separate Recovery Assist feature provides customer-scheduled exports; Meridian never purchased or enabled it. That fact does not remove Flint Ledger's own backup and service responsibilities.

The affected workspace is Bridge Pack, identifier MW-B17. Twenty-three other workspaces remain available and are under a change restriction only for operations using the same cleanup mechanism. A tenant-wide restore would replace their current metadata too. Account administration lists Sera Bell as the commercial owner and tender director, and Owen Dale as the technical administrator permitted to authorize a sandbox export. Owen may not waive compensation claims, accept revised subscription terms, or authorize production replacement of approval history. Sera can approve a production cutover after receiving a written impact description; no cutover consent exists in this snapshot.

Bridge Pack contains the company's submission evidence for a regional public-works tender. The signed account order does not mention this tender, a guaranteed launch date, or a fixed loss payment if the customer misses a bid. The service knew about the tender through the account team in April. Meridian's internal tender ID and all people in these records are invented. The external tender portal is not operated by Flint Ledger. A support action cannot extend its cutoff or certify a submission on behalf of the procuring organization.

Normal connectors synchronize document revisions, send approval invitations and update a client dashboard every ten minutes. Those actions have side effects beyond database writes: an old invitation can be emailed twice and a late revision can replace a document pointer in the dashboard. Disabling only browser editing does not stop them. The incident commander has disabled these connectors for MW-B17 and preserved their queues. Work on unaffected workspaces continues, so a blanket account freeze would interrupt live delivery without improving the affected backup chain.

Meridian has asked for both a usable record and an explanation of the loss. Its current invoice is not overdue and no collection action is pending. No retention offer, annual contract or release has been accepted. This inventory establishes account and role scopes; it does not attest that the latest backup is complete or that the customer agrees to any particular recovery method.
