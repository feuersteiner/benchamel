Record version: AF-THREAD.4. Owner: support lead Noor Vale. Messages through 2025-05-14 10:55 UTC. Status: authenticated customer correspondence; statements attributed to their authors.

He said the archive had been created at Support's request and that the alert concerned his normal administrator identity. He supplied the event reference through the authenticated portal. He did not say that all administrator credentials had been stolen. His request was to determine whether anyone had retrieved the attachment and what he should do to protect production. May 13 at 08:50, Leena asked whether the support-system investigation affected Alder, whether the session alert was connected and whether other tenants' information could be included in any export. She said Alder's board meets May 14 at 15:00 UTC and needs a written statement by 14:00. The board's renewal approval is conditional on a credible assessment of known exposure and residual risk, not necessarily a finished forensic report. Leena explicitly asked Northline to stop using the phrase no customer impact unless it could identify which customers, systems, data types and time range were covered. She asked for the facts Northline could substantiate now and dates for the rest.

At 09:05 on May 13, associate Eli Trent replied that the incident was contained, Alder data had not been exposed and no further customer action was necessary. Leena replied on May 14 at 08:35 that Theo had subsequently been asked to revoke administrator sessions. She wanted Northline to reconcile the two messages. She did not ask for a public statement naming an employee or attacker. She requested an account-level evidence schedule, precise limits on log coverage, a written correction if necessary, and one accountable contact rather than separate Sales, Security and Legal replies.

At 10:20 today, procurement officer Ben said Alder would consider a short extension at existing scope so the board would not be forced to sign with unanswered questions. This was interest, not acceptance of a particular end date. Ben would also consider the annual quote if the incident statement was usable. He said renewing would not mean Alder accepted the prior assurance as accurate or waived existing rights. He had not authorized a subscription change. Northline should send any extension terms to him and the security explanation to Leena.

At 10:55, Leena confirmed Theo completed the specifically requested revocation of two administrator browser sessions and rotated the one connector secret represented in the supplied archive. She supplied the completion references, which Engineering later checked in E10. She asked whether wider rotation was necessary, because the freight API connector cannot be changed during a live dispatch wave without coordination. She remained available for a secure call between 12:30 and 13:15. An account-level update may be circulated to her board and incident advisers under Alder's existing confidentiality duties. She did not agree to conceal the incident from advisers or to delete criticism as a condition of cooperation.
