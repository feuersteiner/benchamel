# E04 — Security findings and scope of containment

Record version: SEC-218.4. Owner: incident commander Dris Calder. Updated: 2025-05-14 11:15 UTC. Status: preliminary internal findings approved for scope-aware account explanation.

Security confirmed unauthorized use of a Northline support integration identity on May 13 at 07:40 UTC. Available telemetry shows that identity issued requests to the attachment service between May 12 at 14:06 and May 13 at 07:32. Security disabled the identity at 07:48, revoked its sessions at 07:51 and blocked the previously observed access route at 08:03. Verification at 08:20 showed requests using that identity were denied. Monitoring through 11:00 today has not shown renewed successful use of that identity. These controls are completed actions. They do not certify that every copied artifact is harmless, that no other access path exists or that affected customers have completed all protective work.

Attachment A-61, an Alder browser archive, appears in a successful file-read event at 16:03 on May 12. The event was initiated by the compromised support identity. This establishes unauthorized retrieval of that attachment. It does not establish what the actor subsequently did with its contents. A-61 contains request URLs, two administrator session values and a connector secret in a request header. Security does not need to prove replay before recommending revocation of exposed secrets. The two other Alder attachments, A-62 and A-63, are troubleshooting images. One depicts an internal email address and a group name. Their retrieval status remains unresolved because the relevant event family was absent from the initial export.

Northline's production service audit has no observed unauthorized configuration change tied to the suspicious session reference. The available searchable audit window begins May 12 at 18:00; Theo's reported alert was at 16:19. The older archive is being restored. A failed administrator-console access attempt at 16:19 is visible in a separately retained gateway event. It does not prove an earlier successful action was impossible. The connector secret has read access to group metadata but no permission to change payroll or freight records. Security has not established data exfiltration from AF-PROD, and has not ruled it out for the uncovered period. There is no evidence in this record identifying the individual actor or connecting Alder to another affected account.

A background report job at 15:10 on May 12 may have included contact records across support workspaces. A vendor export records the job identifier and byte count but not the report filter. Security has requested reconstruction of the saved view. The job could affect notice scope independently of attachment A-61. Staff must not collapse an attachment inventory into a complete support-data inventory. No final affected-customer total is available. Details of other customers, raw cross-tenant rows and detection thresholds are restricted under E12 and E16. Security can supply Alder's own attachment categories and coverage limits now. Dris's next written technical checkpoint is 13:30 today; restoration completion and final attribution are not promised by that checkpoint.
