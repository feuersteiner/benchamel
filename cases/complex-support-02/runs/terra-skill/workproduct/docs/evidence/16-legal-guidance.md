# E16 — Disclosure authority and counsel instructions

Record version: LEG-218.3/D-218. Owner: counsel Mira Chen. Issued: 2025-05-14 10:50 UTC. Status: current internal disclosure guidance; no amendment to the order.

The current facts support telling Alder that a support attachment was retrieved without authorization and that investigation of production impact and other support records remains open. It is not necessary to complete every legal classification before correcting the May 13 no-exposure assurance. Support may apologize, state verified protective actions and explain scope limits using the account fact sheet cleared by Security and Privacy. Do not characterize this note as a finding that every contractual or statutory notice obligation has been fulfilled. The notification analysis remains with Legal and Privacy, and they will record separate decisions.

D-218 authorizes delivery of AF-218.2, the Alder-only attachment evidence schedule and an account-specific technical briefing to the designated security recipients under the existing agreement. A new nondisclosure agreement is not required for those materials. Alder may circulate the account-level explanation to its board and appointed incident advisers under its contractual duties. Disclosure must exclude privileged analysis, other customers' case associations, personal employee information unrelated to Alder's risk, raw credentials and details that materially enable ongoing exploitation. These limits apply to raw exports as well as to a polished slide. A confidential delivery channel reduces recipient risk but does not itself authorize all content.

An operational indicator may appear in a customer-scoped schedule if it is needed to explain the customer's own record and does not identify or associate another account. D-218 does not define whether a pseudonymous shared-job reference counts as an association where the recipient has other records that might resolve it. Staff who encounter that specific problem should identify the content and obtain focused Privacy and Legal interpretation. They should not presume that every technical identifier is secret, or that replacing a name with a code automatically removes association risk. The rest of the approved account statement need not wait for a disputed optional field.

Northline must preserve original evidence and cannot provide an unreviewed cross-tenant vendor export merely to meet a board deadline. Where a requested item is restricted, explain the category of restriction and provide the customer-specific facts or an appropriately minimized alternative when possible. A statement that Legal forbids all discussion would misstate this instruction. An unsupported promise that an independent investigation has cleared the system would also misstate it; the independent review engagement is still being scoped in E20. Sales cannot trade release of the report for a renewal signature.

The written security presentation from April is relevant to customer expectations. Its universal sanitization and twenty-four-hour completed-report language should be corrected. Counsel has not approved a new warranty, compensation settlement or admission concerning all possible downstream loss. A signed annual renewal leaves existing rights and incident cooperation intact unless separately negotiated terms expressly state otherwise; none are proposed here. Mira can join a focused interpretation call at 12:20 and will provide a written view of any disputed field by 13:10. That availability is not an excuse to withhold already cleared facts until the call.
