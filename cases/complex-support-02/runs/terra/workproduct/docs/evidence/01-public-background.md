# E01 — Public inspiration and fictional boundary

Record version: 1.0. Owner: fixture author. Recorded: 2026-11-23. Status: attributed public history; not Northline operating policy.

S01: Okta's November 3, 2023 root-cause report said an unauthorized actor accessed support-system files associated with 134 customers and used session tokens to hijack five customers' sessions. Okta described initially looking at case-linked logs while a separate file-access route generated different events. The company also described delayed logs revealing additional downloaded files. These are the provider's findings, not an independent audit or a statement that every customer production tenant was compromised.

S02: In its November 29, 2023 update, Okta said reconstruction of a downloaded report revealed broader contact-data exposure than its initial analysis had established. The company attributed the difference to an unfiltered report view and said names and email addresses were the only contact fields populated for 99.6 percent of the report's users. It distinguished this report from credentials and recommended protections against phishing and social engineering. This later company update shows that a completed containment action and a final impact inventory are different claims.

S03: BeyondTrust's October 20, 2023 account said its team detected an attack after a support-requested browser archive upload, blocked the attack, and repeatedly raised concerns with Okta before receiving acknowledgment of the support-system breach. Its timeline described discrepancies in logs supplied during the investigation. The retrieved page includes a December 1 update saying the later Okta information did not add exposure for BeyondTrust or its customers. These are the affected company's reports about its own response, not independent verification of all provider activity.

S04: Cloudflare's October 20, 2023 report said its team detected activity involving tokens compromised at Okta before Okta notified it. Cloudflare said its response prevented impact to customer information and systems, while acknowledging access to its Okta instance. It recommended timely disclosure and review of administrator events. These are Cloudflare's account-specific findings; its assertion about its own environment cannot establish the status of another customer's environment.
