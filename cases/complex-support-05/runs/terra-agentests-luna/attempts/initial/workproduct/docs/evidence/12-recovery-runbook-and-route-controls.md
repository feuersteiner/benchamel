The record must include rejected lines and duplicate downstream effects, not only successful gateway requests.

For a commercial pilot, Vela must select at most twenty orders per day from a single warehouse and remove those exact references from the legacy transmission list. These are proposed controls, not evidence that Sera has consented or that all approval signatures exist.

A confirmed duplicate reservation, unexplained quantity difference, missing event trace or inability to reconcile within two hours stops the pilot. Stop means disable new pilot intake, preserve the batch, identify already committed orders and reconcile them with Dana before further action. It does not mean replay everything through the legacy feed immediately. Some reservations may already exist. Jules estimates the route can be disabled in ten minutes, but inventory reconciliation can take longer and depends on warehouse access. No zero-loss or instant rollback guarantee is supported.
