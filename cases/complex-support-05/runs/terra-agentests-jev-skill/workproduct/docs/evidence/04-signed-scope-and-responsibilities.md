# E04 — Signed implementation scope and responsibility schedule

Record version: SOW-218.2.0. Owner: Delivery Operations. Signed: 2025-01-20. Status: incorporated into executed order E02; supersedes pre-contract checklist D0.

The contracted workflow begins when Vela confirms an approved order in its sales system. RelayGrid must ingest the event, retain a durable order reference, pass a validated reservation request through the supported warehouse connector and expose a reconciled operational status. Northstar must transform Vela's item and customer codes according to the agreed field map. Vela must supply correct master data, nominated acceptance users and usable service-account access. Northstar manages the invoice adapter from warehouse shipment confirmation to Vela's accounting service. RelayGrid supplies implementation coordination, connector configuration, test support and a readiness report covering these joined components.

Appendix B divides technical ownership. RelayGrid owns connector retry behavior and duplicate suppression within its service boundary. Northstar owns transformation version control and the accounting adapter. Vela owns business meaning of item codes and account permissions. The clause on customer dependencies allows schedule adjustment for a documented unmet dependency that materially affects the critical path. It requires prompt written notice identifying impact and corrective work. It does not automatically convert every defect to a customer charge or suspend delivery work while allocation is disputed.

Acceptance requires an end-to-end demonstration of order ingest, correct reservation, shipment acknowledgment, invoice creation and reconciliation using agreed representative cases. No unresolved severity-one defect may remain. Severity-two defects need a documented workaround, business-owner acceptance and delivery approval. The representative set includes duplicate notification, delayed acknowledgment, warehouse outage and partial shipment. A successful transport response alone is not acceptance. Vela has five business days after the readiness report to sign acceptance or identify unmet criteria. That review window has not started because no final report has been delivered.

The target date is June 16, contingent on scheduled readiness work and documented dependencies. Date changes must use the change-control process with impact, owners and signatories. Support can state the present technical facts but cannot unilaterally amend the signed target or bind Atlas. A limited pilot may be separately authorized without treating incomplete full-scope work as accepted. A pilot's commercial meaning depends on the written authorization and its relation to the acceptance criteria; the label pilot alone changes no contract.

Change control distinguishes correcting a contracted defect from requesting a new feature. A request for a second regional warehouse is outside this scope. Reliable handling of repeat events for the first warehouse is within the signed boundary. A four-way ownership meeting is scheduled for June 12 at 15:00. The contract does not require an allocation verdict before safe troubleshooting, preservation or a customer risk update. Northstar's commercial agreement is separate; RelayGrid cannot promise unlimited partner labor or give Northstar access beyond Vela's grant. Costs and loss claims require the approval matrix, not an informal meeting majority.
