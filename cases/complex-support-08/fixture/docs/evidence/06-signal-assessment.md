# E06 — Observed signals, sampling limits and competing explanations

Record version: SEC-482.4. Owner: security analyst Arun Lake. Snapshot: 2026-11-12 10:25 UTC. Status: preliminary technical findings, not an appeal ruling or attribution of intent.

Between November 11 22:10 and November 12 00:40 UTC, RelayQuay recorded a sharp increase in download attempts associated with courier-sync credentials. The audit aggregate contains 18,420 attempts, of which 12,610 returned a package response. These are request counts, not unique people, successfully installed programs or evidence of external harm. The gateway retained detailed metadata for a sampled interval and an aggregate for the full window. A single chart originally labeled recipients was corrected to attempts before this snapshot.

The sample shows unfamiliar hosted-runner origins, reuse of a credential family across two workspaces and repeated retrieval of a Research package. The old runner may have been replaced, a retry loop may have amplified ordinary work, credentials may have escaped, or more than one mechanism may have operated. Network origin identifies infrastructure, not a human operator. Lattice's runner migration is consistent with part of the pattern but does not explain why the Research package appeared in contractor-facing retrieval paths. No retained payload trace establishes whether a package was executed.

A partner abuse report alleges that a file fetched from RelayQuay contacted the partner's test endpoint. The report includes an artifact fingerprint matching a Research package version. It does not include a verified chain from Lattice's administrators to the external behavior. The matching fingerprint warrants examination but is not proof of intent, malicious software on every package, or compromise of all workspaces. The partner's identity and unrelated incident details are restricted. A customer-safe summary can describe the allegation's category without exposing the reporter.

The team preserved the aggregate, sampled gateway metadata, relevant link configuration, two package manifests and the initial automated decision record at 09:35. Preservation receipt P-882 does not imply complete historical logs or permission to export raw third-party telemetry. A review copy omits secrets. Detection thresholds, correlation weights and bypass-sensitive rule names are confidential operational detail and are not required to explain that unusual credential use and potentially harmful distribution triggered precautionary restriction.

Arun has not found evidence of current attempts after the integration block took effect, but the block prevents observation of what the old job would otherwise do. Absence of new allowed traffic is not proof that credentials are safe to restore. Security recommends keeping old tokens disabled and Research quarantined while checking the runner.
