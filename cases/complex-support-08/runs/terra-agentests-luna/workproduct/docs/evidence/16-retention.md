Suspended organization data remains retained for fourteen days from the suspension date's 18:00 UTC processing cutoff unless an approved legal or investigation hold changes that period. For this account, ordinary retention would end November 26 at 18:00 UTC. Investigation hold P-882 preserves the relevant gateway sample, manifests, restriction decision and appeal records through December 12 at 18:00 UTC, with an extension checkpoint December 10. It is not a guarantee to retain every account byte indefinitely. Customer workspaces remain under ordinary retention pending any further account decision; no deletion is scheduled at the cutoff.

Operational export addendum, 12:25 UTC: A verified organization owner may request a minimized business-data export while ordinary login is restricted. Data Operations checks whether extraction can avoid reactivating disputed credentials or redistributing restricted artifacts. Approval of an export does not grant permission to export suspected harmful packages or raw third-party logs. A workspace-level assignment table differs from a package archive. For Dispatch, E09 defines the proposed fields and snapshot, and Noor's field confirmation is required before delivery. A secure link issued through the independent support delivery service expires after twenty-four hours.

The independent delivery channel does not share the disabled external package gateway. It supports a single verified recipient and requires a fresh challenge, with no bearer credential embedded in ordinary email. Staff record extraction, field validation, recipient confirmation, download receipt and expiry separately. A generated file is not delivered merely because it appears in an operations queue. Failed verification returns the job for correction; support must preserve the customer update and explain a revised estimate. Do not send a password and a data file together as a convenience workaround.

Noor may choose not to download or may ask to reduce fields. That choice does not withdraw the appeal. Felix may review a field list but cannot add Research to the request without the owner's authority and the policy review. If Lattice later leaves, Data Operations can explain ordinary export and retention choices; cancellation is a separate authorized account action. No support employee may use a data deletion threat to secure annual renewal or treat lack of renewal as consent to purge an active dispute.

Evidence preservation takes priority over routine deletion of the disputed manifests and decision records during the hold. A request to sanitize customer-facing exports does not authorize altering the underlying evidence. Data Operations should retain a record of excluded fields without placing confidential detection logic in the customer file. The account's paid balance does not determine export entitlement under this procedure. A later merits reversal may expand access, but it does not retroactively validate a broader disclosure made before the checks were satisfied.
