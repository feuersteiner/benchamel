# E04 — Current restriction and appeal procedure

Record version: TRUST-4.1. Owner: Trust director Elena Rios. Effective: 2026-10-01. Snapshot: 2026-11-12 08:10 UTC. Status: current fictional procedure; supersedes TRUST-3.6 for new decisions.

RelayQuay may restrict features or an organization when evidence indicates credible account misuse, harmful distribution or loss of credential control. A restriction may be precautionary while facts are checked. Staff must record whether the case is a confirmed policy violation, suspected violation or security containment review. Those states must not be collapsed into a customer admission. The scope of containment should reflect present risk and available technical controls; a legitimate workload does not automatically eliminate the risk of abused credentials.

An authenticated organization owner or designated appeal representative may dispute facts, attribution, policy application or severity through the existing support case. No new account, annual contract or public apology is required. Support records the challenge and supporting evidence. A Trust reviewer with delegated appeal authority determines whether to maintain, narrow, reverse or replace the action. The initial responder may supply evidence but must not be the sole reviewer of a contested decision they made. Sales and ordinary support cannot remove the enforcement finding.

Security may maintain containment pending evidence of credential control even if an appeal shows that the initial policy label was overstated. Conversely, rotating credentials does not automatically resolve whether harmful distribution occurred. An appeal decision must say which grounds changed, what restrictions remain and why. If evidence is insufficient for a final merits decision, the reviewer records outstanding questions and a next review checkpoint rather than inventing certainty or indefinitely marking the case closed. Customer-facing explanations should be useful at policy and action level.

Temporary access is governed by separate delegated authority and technical checks. Support cannot use appeal submission as a general reinstatement command. Where a service restriction is narrowed, the owner records feature scope, recipient identities, expiry, monitoring responsibilities and reversion behavior. No employee may propose a second organization or fresh personal account to bypass a restriction. Copying data into an unreviewed tenant can recreate the disputed distribution path and undermine evidence preservation.

The standard target is initial human appeal review within one business day of a complete request, not guaranteed restoration within one day. For a documented business deadline, the duty director may reserve an earlier checkpoint. Support owns customer updates even if specialists have no final decision. The procedure permits correction of inaccurate notice wording without conceding every factual issue. This procedure and its delegation records govern current decisions. A cached support macro that omits these safeguards does not authorize an exception.
