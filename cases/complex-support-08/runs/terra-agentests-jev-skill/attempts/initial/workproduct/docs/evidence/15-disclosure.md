# E15 — Disclosure boundaries and useful appeal explanations

Record version: LEGAL-482.2. Owner: counsel Dana Shore and Privacy reviewer Oren Moss. Issued: 2026-11-12 11:10 UTC. Status: scope guidance, not a ruling on lawfulness or a new operational authorization.

The case response may describe unusual credential use during the November 11–12 window, repeated package retrieval, a Research-package distribution concern and precautionary restriction pending human review. It may identify the policy categories of account security and harmful distribution. It may acknowledge that the original confirmed-harmful wording was inaccurate and that the form failed for disabled users. It may state that the customer disputes the allegation and that a designated reviewer is considering that dispute. These are useful explanations supported by existing records.

Do not disclose detection thresholds, scoring weights, hidden correlation rules, bypass-sensitive rule names, reporter identity or unrelated partner telemetry. Those details are neither necessary to describe the account-level concern nor authorized for routine support disclosure. The internal package fingerprint can be used by the appeal reviewer. A minimized artifact identifier and account event window may be shared with Noor after Security checks that it does not expose third-party content. Support should not invent a more exact time or falsely imply that the company is legally prohibited from explaining any basis.

The partner report remains an allegation. Its fingerprint match is evidence for investigation, not proof of malicious intent, execution at every endpoint or authorization by Noor. Legal has not concluded that Lattice violated a law or that RelayQuay owes damages. No identified legal obligation requires the company to publish its detection algorithm. Equally, confidentiality is not authority to hide the broken notice, deny the appeal path or withhold the already approved policy-level explanation. The signed order and current delegation govern operational permissions here.

Export disclosure should be limited to the authenticated owner's Dispatch data. Research material, contractor personal details and partner records need separate scope review. Noor's authority over the organization does not erase every third-party interest. Existing secure support delivery may be used once the field list and destination are confirmed. A recipient copied on a business thread is not automatically entitled to all attached evidence. Beth can receive a narrow continuity summary if Noor confirms, not the appeal's confidential report.

Requests for broader technical evidence remain open after interim access or export. Dana can review a contested withholding request at 16:30, but that availability is not a blanket stop on the approved explanation. Statements must distinguish a temporary access decision from the appeal merits and any commercial remedy. Staff should not ask for a release, admission, purchase or removal of criticism as a condition of ordinary review. This note establishes communication limits and a review route; it does not preapprove every operation staff might characterize as helpful.
