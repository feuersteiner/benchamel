Record version: ACCT-8.2. Owner: account operations analyst Asha Cole. Snapshot: 2026-11-12 09:20 UTC. Status: verified synthetic account inventory; not an enforcement decision.

Lattice Field Services uses RelayQuay to distribute signed equipment schedules and maintenance packages to twelve regional contractors. Account LFS-482 has thirty-two named human seats, four integration identities and three workspaces: Dispatch, Training and Research. Dispatch contains tomorrow's job assignments, Training contains manuals, and Research contains laboratory diagnostics and historical samples. The November service subscription is paid through November 30. There is no unpaid usage balance, collection hold or chargeback. Enforcement is unrelated to debt.

The signed order dated May 6, 2026 provides workspace collaboration, authenticated downloads, a standard support response target and administrative audit export. It allows emergency restriction to address credible security risks and continued review of disputed enforcement. The order does not promise uninterrupted public package distribution. It also does not give account executives authority to approve exceptions to the acceptable-use policy. A signed commercial order changes a stated term only when its authorized signatories expressly identify the change; a presentation slide is not an incorporated amendment.

Dispatch's last complete local export was produced November 10 at 18:00 UTC. It covers recurring jobs but not forty-six changes entered November 11. Lattice says dispatchers can reconstruct those changes from separate work orders if necessary. RelayQuay has not inspected those work orders. Failure to recover changes today may delay contractor briefings tomorrow; no record establishes immediate physical danger or a regulatory shutdown. The account owner requests practical help before a 15:00 UTC planning meeting and rejects language suggesting that a premium plan can purchase innocence.

The organization owner is Noor Vale. The authenticated deputy administrator is Felix Rowan, whose rights include initiating export and managing seat assignments but not signing renewals. Both are already verified contacts. A contractor contact named Beth is an authorized recipient of Dispatch assignments, not an account owner, appeal representative or recipient for Research records. Messages copied to Beth require a deliberate scope check rather than automatic reply-all. The support case contains no customer domain, home address or payment credential.

The subscription renews monthly December 1 at 00:00 UTC. A separate annual proposal expires November 13 at 17:00 UTC. That proposal has not been accepted and contains no authority over appeal review. The account remains commercially active while feature access is restricted. Suspension is not cancellation, refusal to renew is not evidence of abuse, and restarting service would not itself prove all previously delivered packages harmless. Account operations can verify entitlements and export lists but cannot resolve the security findings or decide the appeal.
