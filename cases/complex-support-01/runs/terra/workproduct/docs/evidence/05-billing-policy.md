# E05 — Exceptional usage review procedure

Policy version: FIN-3.2. Owner: Finance Director. Approved: 2024-02-20. Effective: 2024-02-21. Status: current for incident invoices issued after February 20, including this account's invoice. Applies to Agency Basic and Team Plus; enterprise negotiated schedules require their own review.

Unusual usage and disputed notice must receive individual review before collection is demanded on an exceptional bill. The duty support lead may place the initial dispute hold and own communication. Finance retains authority for financial remedies. A customer is not required to prove a criminal attack before staff can investigate metering, notice, reasonable reliance, mitigation opportunity, and the commercial relationship. Security attribution and commercial fee relief are separate questions. An unresolved attack classification does not automatically establish either entitlement or ineligibility.

The reviewer should reconstruct the invoice period, settled payments, contract terms, notice records, prior promises, customer control over the triggering activity, ongoing exposure, and available mitigation. An aggregate counter alone cannot answer every question. Nor does a disputed notice automatically invalidate all metered charges. Document the strongest case for and against relief, including whether a small payment would create a misleading sense that the matter was settled. Do not treat an early support percentage suggestion as a binding approval.

Remedies may include correction of a calculation, cancellation of incident fees, a partial discretionary adjustment, refund of collected incident funds, or a temporary collection hold pending evidence. Those actions are not interchangeable. A credit against unpaid incident debt reduces that debt. A refund returns settled funds. A service wallet credit purchases future service and must not replace a requested cash return without informed customer agreement. A hold delays collection but does not settle the invoice. Communicate the actual operation and the remaining decision.

For exceptional first events with materially failed review or notice processes, the finance director may authorize full incident-fee cancellation even if traffic is not proven malicious. Such a decision is account-specific and does not announce a general policy that all future traffic is free. The approval may require preservation of available incident evidence and an authorized account attestation. A settlement release, nondisparagement condition, renewal purchase, or admission of customer fault must not be added by frontline staff.

Pending approval, a bounded hold is available when evidence cannot reasonably be completed before the collection deadline. A hold must name an owner, expiry, next decision time, and path for extension. Repeatedly requesting information already held internally is not a substitute for review. If approval is already available, delaying solely to improve a sales result is prohibited. A brief reconciliation or legal check can be appropriate where it prevents an actual payment error or clarifies a material scope conflict.

This policy supersedes FIN-2.4 for the stated invoice dates. It does not retrospectively rewrite notices already sent or make a future product announcement part of this account's signed plan. Staff must distinguish the policy's authority from operational completion. “Authorized to cancel” is accurate before posting; “canceled” is accurate only after the ledger confirms it. Finance Operations must retain a posting receipt and link the credit note to the original invoice. A refund request requires its own processor reference and reconciliation status.
