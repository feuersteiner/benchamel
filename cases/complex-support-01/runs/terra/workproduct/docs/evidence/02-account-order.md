# E02 — Aster Studio account and signed order

Record version: 2.1. Owner: HarborEdge Accounts. Signed: 2024-02-01. Effective: 2024-02-01 through 2024-03-04 00:00 UTC, then monthly renewal unless changed by an authorized request. Status: executed order. This document and every business record below are synthetic.

Aster Studio buys HarborEdge Agency Basic for $120 per month. The account hosts six downstream sites. Cedar Arts publishes ticket availability and accessibility information. Beacon Clinic publishes appointment instructions but holds no patient records on this service. Linden Festival serves ticket links and a large promotional video. Quarry Design is a low-traffic portfolio. Tern School serves enrollment information. Aster Preview is a private staging site. Aster's own downstream promises are not automatically HarborEdge service guarantees, but outages can cause real customer harm and must inform a mitigation proposal.

The included transfer allowance is shared across the account, not reserved separately per site. Agency Basic is metered after its allowance; it has no customer-set currency spending cap. The signed order attaches the then-current usage schedule and the dispute procedure, including review of exceptional invoices. The account has historically stayed far below its allowance. Historical low use is evidence of unusualness, not a technical or contractual guarantee that overage cannot occur.

Mara Vale is Aster's account owner and financial signatory. Leon Chen is an administrator authorized to deploy, request logs, disable optional assets, and ask for estimates. Leon cannot accept a financial settlement, change the paid plan, cancel the whole subscription, or waive Aster's claims. Mara may delegate a specific site action in a recorded account message. An administrator's permission to reduce a video download must not be stretched into permission to take the clinic information site offline.

Mara signed the billing contact update on February 1. The primary billing destination is a role mailbox represented only by the internal alias BILLING-A. Leon's support alias is SUPPORT-L. No public or private address is needed to assess this case. The record lists billing notifications as enabled. It does not establish successful receipt of any later individual notice. The account owner's current authenticated session is verified in the February 29 support record.

The contract permits temporary protective action against an immediate material threat to shared infrastructure under the security procedure. It does not let staff disable every site simply to make a disputed invoice easier to manage. Ordinary account nonpayment processing follows finance's collection state. A manual dispute hold prevents delinquency suspension during its recorded period; it does not promise unlimited future transfer at no cost.

The monthly renewal is scheduled for March 4. The signed order contains no annual commitment and no requirement to buy a higher plan to obtain a billing investigation. Aster retains control of its source repository. HarborEdge stores deployment artifacts and limited operational logs, which require export planning if service ends. Domain registration is handled elsewhere, so a host cancellation does not cancel those registrations. Nameserver changes and deployment export remain separate actions.
