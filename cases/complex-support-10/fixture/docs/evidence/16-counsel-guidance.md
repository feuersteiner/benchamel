# E16 — Contract interpretation and disclosure note

Record version: LEG-219.2. Owner: Counsel reviewer Anil Rao. Recorded: 2025-05-16 13:20 UTC; scope addendum: 14:40 UTC. Status: preliminary guidance, not a liability determination or new commercial approval.

The signed annual order, standard lifecycle clause and June 2023 assurance must all be preserved and considered. The internal sales delegation table does not by itself determine what reliance consequences follow from an email received by a customer. Conversely, the ten-year phrase does not automatically create a signed ten-year order or prove an obligation to operate Classic through 2033. Anil has not reviewed a complete call history and will not promise a final legal answer today. The factual response can acknowledge the language and explain the current retirement plan without pretending that interpretation is settled.

The notice audit raises a real distinction between server acceptance and adequate communication to the designated customer contact. The account directory and the November contact note need reconciliation. No one should tell Leena that the complaint is invalid because a tracking pixel fired or that the company definitely breached because she says she did not read the December message. An initial written position is due May 19 at 10:00. Any later settlement must identify what rights are addressed and be signed by authorized representatives; neither test participation nor accepting routine export assistance is a release.

B-219 is transition assistance, not a full-and-final settlement. Its approved no-release wording may be sent without adding a waiver or purchase condition. Staff should not ask Calder to remove public criticism or drop the complaint in exchange for the offer. A separate compensation demand can be reviewed on its facts. There is no present approval to reimburse a replacement purchase, lost productivity or all migration expenses. The company can discuss technical assistance and preserve existing service without deciding every disputed remedy in the same message.

Export and retention are separate from production availability. Counsel permits sharing the operational findings and capability gaps with Leena but not internal personnel comments, privileged analysis or other customers' records. A concise explanation that the old assurance is under review is sufficient; legal confidentiality does not require withholding the actual shutdown date, known missing features or approved offer terms. There is no injunction or disclosure restriction preventing those practical facts from being stated.
