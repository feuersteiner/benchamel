# E17 — Transition cost worksheet and independent destination

Record version: REV-219.3. Owner: Commercial Operations, Keira West. Recorded: 2025-05-16 13:35 UTC. Status: planning estimates and approved-price references, not customer selections.

The worksheet lists three different cost categories: continued Classic hosting, successor subscriptions and migration effort. The approved B-219 continuation has a waived $4,000 prospective charge and therefore no additional hosting payment for July and August if accepted within its terms. Flow Premium's optional first-year proposal is $27,000 after the proposed discount, subject to a signed annual order. Northstar's sixteen assistance hours are staff services with no cash value payable to Calder. These categories should not be combined into a refund total or an assertion that all transition costs are covered.

Calder estimates forty to eighty staff hours to review exports and map identities, plus possible training time. Northstar has not validated that estimate. A migration partner quoted $6,500 to configure a competing system, subject to its own assessment; Northstar has not approved paying that invoice or endorsing the partner. If Calder elects an independent destination, Northstar can deliver the documented export and explain the original signature package. The receiving provider and Calder must evaluate their own import behavior. Northstar cannot guarantee another platform's offline functionality or reproduce its contractual commitments.

The worksheet notes a possible lower-priced Flow Standard package but does not recommend it for the field cycle. Standard retains the same offline and lineage limitations as Premium while reducing storage allowance and specialist support. Discounting a product that lacks a mandatory workflow does not cure the mismatch. Sales may present the price if asked, but a cheaper plan should not be described as an equivalent solution or as the only means to retain access to records already purchased under Classic.

A supported independent exit can begin with the authorized export now, followed by Calder's own destination evaluation. If Calder declines the continuation and has no accepted replacement by June 30, it will need an internally approved operational fallback. A PDF archive is not that fallback for creating new inspections. Northstar should explain this consequence without asserting that buying Flow is required. Calder has the authority to choose an imperfect transition, but informed choice requires the operational risk, dates and limits to be visible.

Commercial Operations can prepare comparison figures for the board packet by May 19 at 09:30. The worksheet does not decide whether the older assurance entitles Calder to more assistance; Counsel and authorized signatories own that review. It also does not convert a proposed discount into leverage over the complaint. Keeping the cost categories distinct helps prevent a reply that sounds generous while an internal plan charges for an unaccepted annual order or quietly substitutes staff hours for a requested monetary remedy.
