# E04 — Original sales assurance and signature limits

Record version: CRM-2023.2. Owner: Revenue Operations, Hal Baird. Extracted: 2025-05-15 11:20 UTC. Status: authenticated historical email plus current authority lookup; all content synthetic.

On June 20, 2023 account executive Dorian Lane wrote to Leena: Classic is the dependable ten-year home for your field workflows. Northstar will be there through 2033, and any newer platform will carry the same core field capabilities so your team will not face a forced rebuild. This message followed a demonstration in which offline capture and amendment lineage were explicit customer requirements. Dorian copied the sales engineer but did not copy an enterprise signatory. The attached price workbook calculated an illustrative ten-year ownership cost, with a footnote saying annual pricing remained subject to orders.

On June 22 the sales engineer wrote internally that the ten-year language was too broad for an annual hosted-service order. The engineer suggested replacing it with long-term investment language and recording offline support as a current feature. The CRM contains no sent correction to Leena before signature. It contains a closed task titled expectations clarified, entered by Dorian after a call. No recording or contemporaneous call note establishes what was clarified. It would be unsupported to say the customer was explicitly told that the original assurance had no meaning.

The July 1 signed order in E02 incorporates the master agreement and states that amendments require signatures by designated representatives. Northstar's 2023 authority table allowed account executives to quote standard packages and explain published features. They could not bind product availability beyond a signed order or change lifecycle notice obligations. That table is an internal delegation limit; it is not itself evidence that Leena knew the limit or that no legal consequence can arise from reliance on the email. Counsel is reviewing the relationship between correspondence, signed terms and customer reliance.

The present CRM owner marked the assurance as material to the dispute, preserved the original message and asked that the reply not call it a misunderstanding by Calder. Dorian has left the business and is unavailable for today's call. There is no reason to speculate about intent or fault. The team can acknowledge the language and its inconsistency with the current retirement plan without claiming either that a ten-year contract was signed or that the customer invented the expectation.

The ownership worksheet also includes an estimated future hardware refresh every four years. That estimate is not an entitlement to a hardware reimbursement and has no bearing on the June hosted shutdown unless a later authorized settlement says so. Revenue Operations has restricted internal comments about commission recovery; those comments are unnecessary to explain the available services. The customer needs a reliable statement of the company's present commitments and accountable review, not a personnel narrative.
