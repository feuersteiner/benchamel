# E02 — Account scope and active subscription

Record version: ACCT-219.3. Owner: Account Operations, Sima Vale. Recorded: 2025-05-15 10:00 UTC. Status: verified fictional account and signed order extraction.

Calder Field Services uses Northstar Workflow Classic to coordinate equipment inspections at twelve remote depots. The account has 120 licensed inspectors and eight office reviewers. Eighty inspectors use the offline field client during shifts with intermittent connectivity. Reviewers need the signed inspection trail for customers who challenge a release decision. The account contains 46,200 inspection records, 8,400 attachments and 3,100 linked amendment events as of May 15. The counts are database objects, not people or completed export files. A single inspection can have more than one amendment.

Order N-219 was signed on July 1, 2023 by Calder's procurement director Leena Moss and Northstar's authorized enterprise signatory Salim Ko. The service term runs through June 30, 2025. Calder prepaid $24,000 for the current annual term on July 2, 2024; that invoice is settled with no outstanding debt. The order describes the Classic hosted service and offline field client, with standard support and ordinary security maintenance during the term. It contains no express availability end date beyond the term and no signed commitment to supply Classic perpetually. The interpretation of related correspondence remains under review.

The master agreement permits Northstar to retire a product with at least 180 days' written notice to the designated account contact. It requires reasonable export assistance for thirty days after service termination. A separately signed order can override the standard lifecycle language. Changes to material purchased functionality during an existing term require account review; the agreement does not define every feature change as material. It does not authorize staff to label an imperfect replacement equivalent simply because both products manage inspection forms. No contractual damages or statutory interpretation has been decided in this dossier.

Classic is scheduled for general hosted shutdown June 30 at 23:59 UTC, coinciding with this account's term end. The normal notice record says December 1, 2024, but the contact evidence is incomplete. Renewal is not automatic under this order: a new signed order is required. The draft successor proposal has not been signed and no successor payment has been collected. No one can describe a scheduled sales opportunity as a completed purchase or imply that refusing it makes today's paid service disappear early.

Leena may approve subscriptions, changes in commercial terms and production cutovers. Calder operations lead Owen Price may approve a nonproduction test using sanitized records and request routine exports into Calder's existing secure workspace. Owen cannot accept loss of audit lineage, a contractual release or a new annual purchase. All times in this dossier are UTC. The existing account's secure workspace is already an approved processing destination; an external migration partner is a separate destination requiring review.
