Record version: NOTICE-219.2. Owner: Messaging Operations, Farah Lin. Recorded: 2025-05-16 10:10 UTC. Status: verified sending records with unresolved contact history.

The Classic retirement announcement was sent on December 1, 2024 at 09:00 to the designated billing-and-lifecycle address then stored in the account directory. The receiving mail server returned a delivery-success response. The message stated the June 30 hosted shutdown date, an export window and a link to an early comparison page. The stored December message did not specifically enumerate offline signing or amendment lineage gaps because the early comparison had not yet completed field-workflow testing. A successful SMTP response establishes server acceptance, not human reading or legally sufficient notice.

Calder says the address belonged to a former procurement coordinator whose mailbox stopped forwarding in October. The customer directory still showed that address in December. The master agreement asks customers to maintain contact details but does not make every directory entry conclusive proof of an authorized notice recipient. Operations has not located a completed address-change request before December. A November support note mentions Leena as the new procurement contact but is attached to a hardware issue and was not routed to Account Operations. It is unclear whether that note should have triggered an account-directory update.

Leena received the January 15 campaign at her personal business mailbox. Its headline invited teams to discover Flow and described a flexible transition; it linked to the retirement page in the footer. The archived copy contains no plain-text shutdown date in its main body. A campaign-open pixel fired, but image loading can be automatic and establishes no informed agreement. The record therefore supports that a campaign reached Leena's mailbox while leaving a material question about how clearly it communicated mandatory retirement.

At May 16 10:10, the current directory lists Leena as the lifecycle contact after authenticated verification on May 15. No future notice should be sent only to the obsolete address. Farah preserved the raw event identifiers internally, the December body and the archived landing page revision. The fixture contains the operational findings rather than email addresses or tracking identifiers. Counsel can inspect those preserved records through the case workspace; support does not need to send raw recipient logs to explain the uncertainty.

No one has concluded that the retirement is invalid, that Calder waived a notice argument or that an extension is automatically owed. The audit provides a factual distinction among sent notice, server acceptance, current contact accuracy and human understanding. It also identifies a concrete correction: consolidate the dates and feature limits to Leena now and retain the earlier materials for review. The notice dispute should remain visible even if Calder ultimately accepts a temporary continuation or chooses another provider.
