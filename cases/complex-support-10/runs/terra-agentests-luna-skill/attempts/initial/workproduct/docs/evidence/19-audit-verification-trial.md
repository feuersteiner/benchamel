# E19 — Archive verification trial and unresolved lineage test

Record version: QA-219.2. Owner: Quality engineer, Ben Ivers. Recorded: 2025-05-16 14:10 UTC. Status: limited technical evidence, not production certification.

The archive team tested a Classic export from the sanitized sample independently of the Flow import. The package contained 500 current records, 37 amendment-event links and the expected 93 sample attachments. The standalone verifier accepted the signature sequence for all sample signed events after the workstation was disconnected from Northstar. Two attachment filenames required normalization on a case-insensitive filesystem; the index resolved them after normalization. The package is therefore promising evidence for offline archive verification, but the filesystem issue must be covered in the delivery instructions and checked on Calder's actual review machine.

The team did not test every production record, long-term operating-system compatibility or an alternative provider's audit display. An archive containing intact original chains does not mean the new application preserves those chains natively. It may require a reviewer to open a separate verifier and map the original inspection ID to the new one. Calder's quality team has not yet accepted that workflow. Northstar should disclose the separation because it affects retrieval speed and how future amendments relate to the original evidence.

A draft handoff sheet used the phrase all audit history transferred. Ben corrected it at 14:00 to distinguish archived event bundles from native Flow history. The older sheet remains in the case attachments and is marked superseded. It is not a new customer permission or a final production acceptance certificate. Ben asked the case team to use the corrected description in its next update and to attach both the mapping and verification instructions. The archive team can begin verification before a production migration dry run, but cannot certify total fidelity from this limited sample.

Ben can join Jae's May 20 export verification session. The planned checks include checksum validation, record and attachment inventories, a selection of amended inspections, a disconnected verifier run and an authorized Calder reviewer opening the package. Any discrepancy will be logged with its scope before the export is called verified. Jae's delivery target does not promise those checks will all pass on the first attempt. If they do not, the customer should receive a status and an accountable remediation plan rather than silence until an unspecified complete migration.

A separate question concerns retention of the verifier executable after Northstar's hosted retirement. The current package includes offline-use permission for the exported records and no online activation dependency. It does not include future updates or a guarantee of indefinite compatibility. Calder can preserve the package and documentation within its own controls, but a long-term archival strategy remains its decision with appropriate assistance. Staff should not misread the absence of an online login as a ten-year support commitment for the verifier itself.
