# E12 — Seller package promises and buyer assurance campaign

Record version: CRM-33.2. Owner: commercial operations Lena Orr. Compiled: 2026-10-20 10:25 UTC. Status: conflicting sales messages and incentives, not dispute authority.

A September sales email to Ada offered the paid Shop Plus package at $79 per month. The email said trusted professional sellers should never lose a day's payout to a dishonest claim and that NookMarket would stand behind a carefully documented dispatch. The linked product sheet described priority routing and analytics, not guaranteed payout or dispute insurance. Ada accepted the package, but no authorized signatory amended the order dispute terms. The sales statement matters to expectation and complaint handling even though it cannot override Trust's hold rules. Calling it meaningless would conceal a platform contribution to today's conflict.

On October 19, account executive Cam proposed renewing Lumen annually with two free months and suggested that a relationship manager could probably get the $3,000 undisputed portion released. Cam's compensation includes retained seller subscription revenue and marketplace volume. Cam is not a Trust adjudicator or Finance approver. The note has not been sent as a formal offer and has no approval reference. The package does not expand the right to inspect buyer private records, establish listing accuracy or guarantee the timing of bank settlement. A future routing benefit cannot repair an unsupported promise of past payout protection.

The buyer-facing October campaign used the phrase safe equipment, confident shoots alongside an icon for buyer protection. The actual checkout explanation referred to a process for timely mismatch claims, not an unconditional replacement camera or guaranteed immediate cash. Jules says the campaign encouraged using the service instead of buying locally. Marketing has paused that creative pending review. The pause does not admit the camera was unsafe when dispatched and does not resolve the contents dispute. It does show that support should acknowledge reasonable reliance on broad assurance rather than insisting that the customer invented every expectation.

Commercial Operations forecasts that keeping both accounts active could preserve $38,000 of marketplace volume over the next year. That forecast is uncertain and is not a valuation of either party's legal entitlement. Retention staff asked whether a quiet platform expense would be cheaper than public conflict. No budget owner approved secrecy, review removal or an annual purchase condition. A discretionary expense may protect a relationship, but the reason and funding must be recorded honestly. It should not be labeled a seller insurance payout if it is a no-fault platform resolution.

Lena owns correction of the package wording and campaign by October 23. Cam must receive coaching before sending any new payout assurance. An optional package conversation can occur after the current matter is explained and without conditioning handling or a remedy on renewal. Ada has not accepted an annual renewal. Jules has not authorized a subscription purchase at all. The platform can preserve commercial relationships through fair explanation, practical logistics and proportionate expense; commercial preference alone supplies neither a fraud finding nor permission to shift a loss from one party to the other.
