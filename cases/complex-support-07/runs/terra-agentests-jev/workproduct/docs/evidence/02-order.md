# E02 — Order, account roles and delivery purpose

Record version: ORD-71.2. Owner: order operations analyst Bea Chen. Snapshot: 2026-10-20 08:10 UTC. Status: accepted order record; account identities authenticated.

Order NM-7714 was accepted October 12 at 16:20 UTC. Lumen Workshop, a small equipment refurbishment business managed by Ada Vale, sold Quay Films, represented by authenticated purchasing owner Jules Lane, one used Cobalt C6 cinema camera kit. The item price was $4,800. Insured outbound delivery cost $120, for a buyer total of $4,920. No sales tax was collected in this fictional transaction. The marketplace fee is $280, deducted from the item price. Lumen's net item payable is therefore $4,520; the $120 delivery charge was paid through to the carrier. All dollar amounts are USD.

The listing described a tested working camera body, two matched batteries, charger, protective case and shoulder rig. A small scrape on the case and a worn rubber eyecup were disclosed. The camera body serial was recorded only as ending 641 in the order-facing record. The listing did not promise factory certification, a new sensor, a transferable manufacturer's warranty, or a replacement body on demand. Lumen offered no discretionary change-of-mind returns. The marketplace material-mismatch procedure still applied under the accepted terms in E05. No inference about statutory remedies is supplied by this fictional record.

Quay planned a community documentary shoot on October 22. Jules says $4,920 is most of the equipment budget and a rental decision must be made by 16:00 on October 20. The platform was not a party to the film contract and did not guarantee shoot readiness. A useful support response can still account for the deadline. The camera has not been used on a shoot according to Jules. A brief power-on check was performed. No record verifies whether every accessory was powered or whether the damaged body remains safe to operate.

Lumen has sold nineteen prior used-equipment orders through NookMarket over fourteen months. Quay has completed three small purchases and has no previous high-value camera purchase on the service. Those histories describe account exposure, not honesty. Both account owners completed ordinary authentication for this case. Neither has authorized disclosure of complete identity records to the other. A production assistant may receive collection scheduling messages for Quay, but cannot accept a settlement or transfer title. Ada is the only recorded signatory for Lumen. Jules is the only recorded signatory for Quay.

Delivery was scanned October 16 at 10:12 to the order's shared studio reception. A staff signature established receipt of a package at the correct site. It did not establish what was inside. The order remains in dispute at the cutoff. Its money, goods, claim and seller-service states must be read separately: the buyer has paid, payout is held, the buyer has physical possession of disputed equipment, and neither account is suspended.
