# E06 — Expired rapid-close playbook

Record version: DSP-3.1. Owner: archived Support Enablement. Effective through 2026-08-31; retained archive stamp October 20 08:50 UTC. Status: superseded, not current authority.

The old playbook used a simplified photo-first workflow for purchases below $5,000. An associate could recommend a buyer refund when a clear mismatch photograph was supplied, with seller recovery handled afterward. The same playbook suggested using delivery scans and seller tenure to close some low-risk claims quickly. Those shortcuts were operational recommendations under an earlier authority structure; they are not current permissions merely because the order happens to fall below $5,000. The September policy introduced explicit custody, evidence response and signatory checks following internal complaints about one-sided decisions.

An archived macro says that premium sellers enjoy fast payout with protection against buyer abuse. Its examples concern low-value orders already cleared by risk review, not every disputed order. The macro does not define a universal insurance promise, and it never authorizes a sales representative to release a payable. The legacy seller dashboard still links to this text from a training tooltip. Product has acknowledged that stale link and scheduled a correction. The presence of the link is relevant to what Ada might reasonably have understood, even though the accepted checkout terms are current DSP-4.0.

A second archived macro tells buyers that protection means refunds within two business days. The financial department removed that phrase in August because submission, processor acceptance and bank credit were being conflated. Eli used an adapted version in E03 without checking its retirement marker. Support Enablement records confirm the agent copied an old saved response, rather than receiving a new guarantee from Finance. No conclusion about deliberate deception is supported by that fact. The platform still owns the misleading assurance and should correct it in a way the buyer can use for planning.

The retired process did not require a neutral intake when the item's identity was disputed. Its internal review found that photographs could establish an allegation without proving when or by whom a substitution occurred. DSP-4.0 therefore calls for proportionate evidence handling rather than an automatic presumption that a photograph is conclusive. It does not reverse the presumption so that every buyer must be treated as a possible criminal. The old and new documents can explain the confusion while remaining different sources of authority.

Archived staff examples include seller-funded refunds, wallet incentives and account restrictions. None has a case-specific approval for NM-7714. Their amounts, roles and timing do not transfer to this dispute. An archive record should remain available for the explanation of prior messaging and quality review, but should not be copied into a customer reply as current terms. Policy Operations assigned remediation of both stale links to Lena Orr by October 23. That future content correction does not itself protect either party's money today, and does not settle the disputed contents of the parcel.
