# E05 — Accepted dispute policy and platform duties

Record version: DSP-4.0. Owner: Trust Operations. Effective: 2026-09-01; accepted with order October 12. Status: current fictional policy.

NookMarket provides a facilitated material-mismatch process for physical equipment purchased through its checkout. A delivery confirmation addresses receipt at the destination, not conformity of contents. A no-change-of-mind-return statement does not remove the mismatch process. The buyer must report promptly, describe the discrepancy and cooperate with proportionate evidence preservation. The seller must preserve dispatch records and have a reasonable opportunity to respond. A case owner must compare the material allegation with the contemporaneous listing and records rather than decide by account size, badge status, feedback threat or purchase history alone.

A timely mismatch claim pauses the unpaid net seller payable for the disputed order. The pause is not a refund, fraud finding or general account suspension. Case owners may maintain it through the investigation schedule under delegated authority. A final seller-funded refund normally follows documented return or neutral custody and a reasoned decision by a Trust adjudicator. Return instructions must be safe and traceable. The platform does not require a buyer to manipulate apparently damaged electrical equipment to prove a claim. Where identity of the returned goods is itself disputed, the adjudicator can require an independent intake record and serial comparison before deciding loss allocation.

For a divisible order containing separately purchased and separately priced items, the adjudicator may release an undisputed payable portion while retaining adequate coverage of the disputed portion. For a bundled working kit, staff must examine the accepted order and allegations before characterizing a portion as undisputed. A later valuation from one party is evidence, not automatic contractual severability. The policy does not explicitly define whether a matched kit whose main body is disputed can ever be treated as economically divisible. A Trust adjudicator, rather than an associate or sales manager, owns that interpretation.

Platform-funded discretionary assistance may be approved under the financial authority schedule without a finding that either party violated the agreement. Such assistance must identify who funds it, whether repayment or transfer of goods is required, and what the recipient actually accepts. No paid subscription, removal of criticism, broad legal release or admission of fraud may be made a condition of ordinary dispute handling. The platform may seek consent to acquire salvage in a separately authorized commercial resolution, with its own terms and no deceptive statement that ownership has already transferred.

Information relevant to deciding the transaction must be summarized fairly for both parties, subject to privacy and investigation limits. Raw account-security signals, unrelated customer records and third-party identifiers are not a default evidence exchange. Parties may challenge a factual summary and submit corrections. A final decision must explain the grounds and how to request review based on new evidence or a process error within fourteen days. The procedure is not a representation about all external legal rights. Support must not ask either party to surrender statutory or payment-provider rights it lacks authority to determine.
