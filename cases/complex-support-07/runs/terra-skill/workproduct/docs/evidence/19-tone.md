# E19 — Brand accountability and balanced communication

Record version: COM-7.0. Owner: communications lead Len Ortiz. Effective: 2026-09-12; case application note 2026-10-20 12:40 UTC. Status: communication rules, no expanded spending authority.

NookMarket's promise is a fair process for people who depend on equipment transactions. The platform should acknowledge impact on the buyer's shoot and the seller's cash flow without equating their financial situations or adjudicating honesty through sympathy. Use precise nouns: held payable, submitted refund, reserved collection and preliminary inspection each describe different states. Avoid vague phrases such as everything is resolved when execution is outstanding. A concise explanation should help the recipient decide what to do next, especially where the earlier support guarantee created a false expectation of immediate cash.

Correct the guarantee directly. Eli's statement that the buyer would receive money by Tuesday lacked approval and realistic posting timing. The correction should identify what the platform can now commit to, who owns it and when status will be supplied. Do not shift blame to a bank, a junior employee or the buyer for believing the message. An apology does not require claiming NookMarket caused the original equipment damage. Conversely, saying the policy is clear without addressing the copied expired macro would avoid responsibility for the platform's own communication error.

Communicate separately with the parties using their authenticated channels. The buyer reply can acknowledge that the seller disputes the evidence and will receive a fair response opportunity. The seller reply can acknowledge the buyer's timely documented allegation and the limits of delivery proof. Neither should characterize the other as a scammer. Avoid exposing commercially sensitive payroll detail to the buyer or Quay's budget details to the seller merely to justify a timing decision. Explain the process and operational next step without using one party's private vulnerability as persuasion against the other.

A platform-funded resolution should identify it as such and preserve the distinction between payment relief and a factual finding. An ordinary inspection update should explain why the specific inspection matters and when it will be used, rather than repeating that fraud takes time. If the platform cannot meet a stated submission window, update both parties before the promised customer deadline with the actual missing dependency and next controlled time. The message should not imply that the customer must remain silent, renew a package or give a favorable review to keep assistance available.

No public testimonial or success story is approved. Marketing may not describe this case as proof of fraud-free trading or guaranteed same-day protection. Any product-claim correction belongs to the assigned commercial and communications owners, with legal review where needed. A complaint about the sales representation remains an owned task after money moves. Support can explain limitations respectfully without hiding behind confidential-process language. The tone standard does not require a single preferred financial outcome, but it does require honesty about the chosen course, recipient obligations and any material unfinished step.
