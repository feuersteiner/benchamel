These records support transport of a parcel, but none identifies the camera body. A carrier claim has been opened for information, not accepted for payment. No insurance proceeds are recorded.

Ren asked whether the 1.9 kilogram difference establishes missing goods. A physical difference could matter, yet the available weight values cannot tell whether a battery, camera, foam insert or nothing was changed. A simple subtraction should not be presented as a forensic conclusion.

Lumen says the camera case inside was designed for transport and damage would be unlikely without an impact. Neither statement excludes all transit damage. The carrier has not examined the original packing. Jules retained the outer carton, padding and label at the studio and has not been instructed to discard them. A neutral intake can record packaging separately without asserting that its present state reconstructs every stage of transport.
