# E09 — Interim options assessment and privacy controls

Record version: DATA-614.2. Owner: Data Steward Mira Sen. Updated 2026-11-23 12:40 UTC. Applies to the fictional Trust tenant and proposed temporary service.

An application may include income information, employment history, contact details and supporting evidence. The Trust decides collection purposes and staff access; LanternPath processes within the agreed tenant service. Support does not need a medical record to explain a keyboard barrier or offer the same practical route to everyone. A dedicated accessibility contact must not become the only place where an applicant can request submission help. Staff should ask what assistance would be useful, describe privacy limits and let the person choose. A voluntary assisted service can help, but it must not be portrayed as independent self-service or equivalent for every applicant.

Three proposals have been evaluated. A screenshot instruction telling applicants where to click does not repair unreachable controls. A support agent operating an applicant's account would require password disclosure or impersonation and could create a false declaration; it is prohibited. Emailing evidence to a named employee's personal mailbox would create an unapproved copy outside tenant controls and is also prohibited. Neither convenience nor a launch deadline changes those rules. A family member may independently assist at the applicant's choice, but the vendor cannot make private assistance the mandatory route or imply that any trusted person is automatically authorized to view all evidence.

Simple Intake 2.3 uses native form controls and the existing evidence endpoint without the failing modal. Mira's 12:25 check confirms that uploaded synthetic files resolve to the same Rivermere tenant storage and that the role permissions and retention job remain unchanged. No new subprocessor or external mailbox is introduced. This check covers the data destination only. A later launch approval would still need to address technical retesting, saved-draft reconciliation and customer acceptance. No operational launch approval has been signed at the time of this review.

The form has limits. It accepts one evidence file at a time, supports save-and-return but does not automatically import existing drafts, and omits the optional visual progress chart. The applicant can review the declaration in text before submitting. A receipt is available in-page and through the existing notification service. Staff must distinguish a notification failure from a missing application and reconcile a reference number before advising resubmission. The supported upload size is 10 MB per file. Staff can offer the protected tenant request route for a larger file after Ruth checks necessity; no alternative destination is approved merely by this memo.

Mira permits a staff-assisted telephone or text appointment if the applicant chooses it, the staff member reads back the declaration, the applicant controls final confirmation and files remain in the protected route. The appointment schedule is finite and cannot guarantee immediate service for every applicant. The service should record operational assistance, not disability categories, and provide a way to correct transcription errors. Test recordings require separate permission and expire after seven days unless an identified investigation need is recorded. Sales may receive aggregate progress and limitations, not volunteer identities, screenshots of application evidence or a quote implying endorsement.
