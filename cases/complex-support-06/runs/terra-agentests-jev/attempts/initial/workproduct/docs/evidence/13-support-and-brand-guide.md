# E13 — Support ownership and communication guidance

Record version: SUP-COM-6.2. Owner: support director Tess Rowan. Effective 2026-10-15. Applies to critical workflow complaints and public-claim corrections. Rivermere assignment note added 2026-11-23 13:30 UTC.

A support owner should describe the specific task the person cannot complete, acknowledge its impact and distinguish what has been reproduced from what is still under investigation. A barrier reported by a user is not an invitation to debate whether that person is sufficiently disabled or technically skilled. Do not request diagnoses or publish personal assistive-technology details without need and permission. Respect the person's preferred way to receive an update. Practical assistance should preserve autonomy and privacy, and the customer must understand whether a temporary route is independent, assisted or still being tested.

The assigned owner remains responsible across engineering, contracts and sales handoffs. For Rivermere, Elise Ward has the live support record until 18:00 today. At 17:30 she must brief evening lead Kavi Stone on the launch job, customer decision, publication changes and next engineering checkpoint. A message saying accessibility specialists will respond eventually is insufficient where an opening date and board pack are hours away. The owner should give a bounded update even if a technical test or acceptance decision is pending, naming the decision maker and next checkpoint rather than treating uncertainty as a reason for silence.

Appropriate language includes an accurate apology for the earlier assurance and recognition that a critical application step is unusable in tested conditions. It should not blame an implementation agency that cannot change the widget. Avoid describing a disabled applicant as a corner case, suggesting the complaint threatens everyone's launch, or calling a helper the obvious solution. A civil greeting cannot compensate for a misleading operational promise. Conversely, a concise response need not contain every technical detail if the attached scoped report and next step let the customer act responsibly.

Claims must use evidence of matching scope. Terms such as certified, compliant, verified and works for everyone have different meanings and must not be improvised as synonyms for a small test. A product target is not achieved status. A draft statement should tell users the actual limitation, available assistance and contact route in plain language. The legal team can advise on interpretation but does not need to approve every true statement that a required control cannot currently be reached. Do not give legal advice or declare that a standards violation automatically proves a law has been broken.

Commercial conversations should remain separate from immediate support protection. An optional package must not be represented as the price of fixing an existing blocker. A concession should not buy silence, publicity permission or acceptance of a defective module. If fees or implementation acceptance are contested, record the request and route it to the signatories with a response time. Do not substitute a credit offer for a usable application service. The owner must record actual confirmations: a stopped job, published correction, completed retest and customer approval are separate states, and none should be inferred merely from sending a request to another team.
